### What Fills the Gap

The two boron ester molecules are geometrically
interesting precisely because they are not ethers.
They have oxygen coordination sites but the
boron center fundamentally changes the
coordination geometry compared to an ether oxygen.

```
Trimethyl borate:  B(OCH3)3
  Three oxygen sites, each weakened by boron
  Lewis acid character
  HOMO = -7.72 eV (weaker donor than DME at -6.87)
  Stronger donor than fluorinated diluents
  Can enter Li+ first shell as a distinct
  coordination species

Triethyl borate:   B(OC2H5)3
  Same geometry, larger alkyl groups
  Slightly weaker donor (more steric shielding)
  HOMO = -7.64 eV
```

If trimethyl borate enters the Li+ first shell
as a distinct coordination species — alongside
DME-type coordination and FSI coordination —
it adds a third configuration to the population.

Three distinct configurations → n_sig = 3 →
SCE in the 1.45–1.55 range → SCE* zone.

**This is the mechanism FEC uses, applied to a
boron ester instead of a fluorinated carbonate.**
FEC adds a coordination mode to DME.
Trimethyl borate may add a coordination mode
to DME in the same way — without the carbonate
decomposition pathway.

### Why This Has Not Been Explored

Boron esters are used as electrolyte additives
but not as primary solvents or co-solvents in
the ether-based Li metal electrolyte literature.
Their role has been understood as anion receptor
(boron binds FSI⁻) not as Li+ coordination
modifier. The SCE framework predicts a different
function: by entering the Li+ first shell as a
distinct oxygen-donor species, they contribute
a coordination configuration that raises SCE
from the DME baseline toward SCE*.

This is a new mechanistic role for boron esters
in ether electrolytes. It has not been framed
this way in the literature.

```
  2. What is the optimal DME:borate ratio?
  3. Is the borate stable against reduction
     at the Li metal anode?
  4. Does the B-O bond cleave under reductive
     conditions to form B-containing SEI?

Question 4 may actually be an advantage:
  B-containing SEI components (LiBxOy) are
  known to be ionically conductive and
  mechanically compliant. If borate reduction
  contributes to SEI formation, this may
  synergize with the coordination diversity
  mechanism rather than competing with it.
```

### The Silicon Ester Parallel

Trimethoxymethylsilane (CO[Si](C)(OC)OC) follows
the same geometric logic as the boron esters:

```
Si center weakens each methoxy oxygen donor strength
HOMO = -7.63 eV (coordinating range)
Three oxygen sites available for Li+ interaction
Si-O bond cleavage under reduction forms SiO2-type
SEI components — known to be stable

Predicted behavior: similar to trimethyl borate
but with different SEI chemistry
```

Both boron and silicon esters occupy the same
geometric position in the SCE* gap. They are
the non-ether, non-carbonate, non-fluorinated
coordination modifiers that MU places between
DME and THF/DOL in coordination space.

## One-Paragraph Summary

Search 2 confirmed that the SCE* = 1.466 gap
between DME (SCE=1.240) and THF/DOL (SCE=1.528/
1.606) is structurally real and unpopulated by
conventional ether molecules in MU's database.

fluorinators — consistent with the structural
overlap between the DME linear chain and the
fluorinated diluent class, but geometrically
wrong for the coordination modifier role.
The only non-fluorinated, non-carbonate molecules
in the return were trimethyl borate (COB(OC)OC,
score 9.38, HOMO −7.72 eV) and triethyl borate

commercially available, published in other
contexts, and never tested as Li+ coordination
modifiers in an ether electrolyte system designed
toward SCE*. These molecules occupy the correct
electronic position between pure ether donors
and fluorinated non-donors, and their oxygen
coordination sites can enter the Li+ first shell
as a distinct configuration, producing the
three-species population (n_sig=3, dom%≈38%)
that the framework requires at SCE*=1.466.
This is Candidate 3: LiFSI 1.0–1.2M in
DME:trimethyl borate, a Lewis acid coordination
modifier route to SCE* that was not in the
preprint and has not been designed toward from
first principles in the literature.
