## The Cooling Physics — Why Cold Air Is Everything

```
Server cooling works on a simple principle:

  Heat flows from hot to cold.
  Servers are hot.
  If something colder than the servers
  is nearby, heat flows to it.
  The servers stay cool.
  The computation continues.

In warm climates:
  Outside air is warm.
  Warmer than the acceptable
  inlet temperature for servers.
  Mechanical cooling required.
  Chillers. Cooling towers.
  Refrigerant cycles.
  Energy-intensive. Expensive.
  Adds 30-40% to energy bill.
  Generates additional heat that
  must itself be rejected.
  The problem compounds.

In cold climates:
  Outside air is cold.
  Colder than server inlet temperature.
  Always. Year-round.
  Air handling units draw outside air in.
  Pass it through the server halls.
  Exhaust the warmed air outside.
  No mechanical refrigeration.
  No chillers.
  No refrigerant.
  Just: cold air in, warm air out.
  Physics does the work.
  For free.

THE PUE METRIC:

  PUE — Power Usage Effectiveness.
  Ratio of total facility power
  to IT equipment power.
  PUE of 1.0: perfect. Every watt
    goes to computation.
  PUE of 2.0: for every watt of
    computation, one watt wasted
    on cooling and infrastructure.

  Typical warm-climate data center:
    PUE 1.4-1.6.
    40-60% overhead.

  Cold-climate data center with
  free air cooling:
    PUE 1.1-1.2.
    10-20% overhead.

  The difference:
    A 100MW cold-climate facility
    at PUE 1.15 uses 115MW total.
    The same workload in a
    warm-climate facility at PUE 1.5
    uses 150MW total.

    35MW difference.
    Every hour. Every day.
    306,600 MWh per year wasted
    on cooling in the warm-climate site
    that the cold-climate site
    does not waste.

    At $0.05/kWh: $15 million/year.
    Wasted. On moving heat around.
    That the cold climate gets for free.

  The cold climate PUE advantage
  alone — separate from energy cost —
  saves tens of millions per year
  per large facility.
```
