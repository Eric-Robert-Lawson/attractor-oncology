## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The author of The Cloud of Unknowing (c.1375–1390)
is identified in scholarship as an anonymous
Carthusian. LALME places the authorial dialect at
LP 970 — NW Nottinghamshire, the exact zone of
Beauvale Charterhouse. J.P.H. Clark (1977) proposed
a Beauvale Carthusian as the author.

The prior of Beauvale named "Robert" is attested in
1372 and 1381 (VCH Nottinghamshire). His full name
— including any surname — is recorded in British
Library Add MS 27984, folios 9b and 10, which are
the specific source documents the VCH editors used
to attest him.

No published scholarship has identified him by
surname. This research has exhausted every online-
accessible archive and reached the boundary of
what can be derived without physical document access.

The hypothesis — that he was Robert de Wydmerpole,
from the Widmerpole gentry of Nottinghamshire — is
the highest-probability reconstruction from the
available convergent evidence, but is not confirmed.

### CRITICAL NOTE ON VCH PRIOR LIST
The VCH lists "William de Spalding, elected 1370"
and then "Robert, occurs 1372." Walter (1367) is
NOT in the VCH list. This creates a problem:

Either:
(a) The VCH list is incomplete — Walter is missing
    from it, which is confirmed by DE/P/T4491
(b) William de Spalding and Walter are different
    men serving in overlapping periods
(c) Walter resigned before 1370, William was
    elected, and Robert succeeded William c.1372

The sequence most consistent with the evidence:
Walter (1367) → possibly William de Spalding
(elected 1370, brief tenure) → Robert (1372)

OR: Walter = William de Spalding (two names for
the same prior — a religious name and a surname)

THIS AMBIGUITY IS UNRESOLVED. It requires
examination of Add MS 27984 to clarify.
