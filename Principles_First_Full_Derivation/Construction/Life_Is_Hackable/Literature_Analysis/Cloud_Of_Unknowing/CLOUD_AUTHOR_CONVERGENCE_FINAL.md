prior of Beauvale Charterhouse, north-west

his appointment to Beauvale, whose authorial
dialect is independently confirmed by LALME

identification as Beauvale's Carthusian author

corroborated by LALME and by Clark's prior

recoverable fact, held in two undigitised
or un-name-indexed archives.
