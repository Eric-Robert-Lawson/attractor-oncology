in formal records was "Robert de [place of

In the 14th century, Carthusian monks were
typically known within the order by first
name only, with the place of origin used
for formal external documents.

The sources that would carry Robert's full name:

  SURVIVING BUT UNDIGITISED:
  - BL Add MS 6060 (Beauvale cartulary, c.1486):
    Most likely to contain his full name as

    administrative records during his priorship.
  - York Archbishop's Register (Neville,
    1374–1388): Digitised but not name-indexed;
    requires palaeographic search for
    "Beauvale" entries.

  NOT SURVIVING:
