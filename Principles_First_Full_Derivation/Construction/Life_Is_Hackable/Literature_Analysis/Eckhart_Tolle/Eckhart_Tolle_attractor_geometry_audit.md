## PART II: THE NAVIGATOR TYPE ASSESSMENT
## Primary Diagnostic — Executed Before Content Analysis

### 2.1 The Biographical Record

Eckhart Tolle was born Ulrich Leonard Tölle
in 1948 in Lünen, Germany. He studied literature
and languages at the University of London and
then pursued postgraduate research at Cambridge.
At 29, in 1977, he was in a period of intense,
chronic depression and suicidal despair.

One night, the following occurred — in his own
description:

> "I cannot live with myself any longer."
>
> Followed by the recognition: "Am I one
> or two? If I cannot live with myself,
> there must be two of me: the 'I' and the
> 'self' that 'I' cannot live with."
>
> Then: the question became so absurd that
> the mind — unable to find an answer —
> stopped. The identification with thought
> dissolved. What remained was a state
> of deep peace that persisted.

For the next two years, Tolle lived in what he
describes as a state of uninterrupted bliss and
peace, sitting on park benches in London, with
no occupation, no income, no institutional
affiliation. He was functionally homeless and
experienced this as entirely satisfactory.

Over the following decade (roughly 1979-1994)
he began to work informally as a counselor and
teacher, guiding individuals through the same
transition. In 1994 he moved to Vancouver. In
1997 The Power of Now was self-published in an
edition of 3,000 copies. By 2007, following
Oprah Winfrey's endorsement, it had sold
over 2 million copies in North America alone.
