### 11.1 What the Geometric Reading Does Not Determine

**The Kybalion's authenticity.** The Kybalion (1908) was published as "by Three Initiates" and is of uncertain provenance. The seven principles as systematized in the Kybalion may not accurately represent the tradition's actual historical self-understanding. The geometric audit uses the Kybalion as a convenient systematization while acknowledging this uncertainty. **The Paracelsus assessment's limits.** The claim that Paracelsus's Archaeus concept is an early version of the framework's coherence gradient field signal is a structural analogy, not an identity claim. Paracelsus's specific therapeutic methods (his use of mercury, sulfur, and salt as the three primes; his astrological medicine) are not validated by the geometric framework simply because his underlying theoretical concept maps well onto the framework's vocabulary. The structural correspondence is real. The specific medical claims require independent evaluation.
