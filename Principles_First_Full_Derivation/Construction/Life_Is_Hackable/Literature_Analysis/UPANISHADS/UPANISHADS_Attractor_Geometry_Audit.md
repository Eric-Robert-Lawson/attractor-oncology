Adi Shankaracharya (788-820 CE) wrote commentaries

  1. The Brahman/Atman identity as the absolute
     philosophical thesis: there is only one reality

     of Maya; the individual self (Atman) is not

     It is a formal philosophical category: functionally

     pure reality or pure unreality.

  3. The three-level reality framework (Tripitaka):

    - Pratibhasika (apparent): pure superimposition

  4. Adhyasa (superimposition) as the formal mechanism

  5. Viveka (discernment) as the primary instrument:
     the ability to discriminate between sat (real)

     between Brahman and Maya. Viveka is the detection
     instrument's cognitive component — the structural

  6. The four qualifications (Sadhana Chatushtaya)

### 8.2 The Maitreyi Dialogue as Complete Instrument Delivery

Context: Yajnavalkya is about to leave for the
forest (the traditional renunciate withdrawal,
Stage 6 in its renunciate form). His wife Maitreyi
asks: "If I had all the wealth of the earth,
would I achieve immortality through it?"

Yajnavalkya: "No. You would live the life of the
wealthy. But there is no hope of immortality
through wealth."

Maitreyi: "What use to me is wealth if I cannot
attain immortality through it? Teach me instead
what you know about immortality."

This is the terminal condition crisis in its
most compressed form: Maitreyi has identified
the authored landscape's best case (wealth,
comfort, security) as structurally insufficient
for what she actually wants. She is at basin
edge. She is ready. The instrument can be delivered.

Yajnavalkya then delivers the core teaching:

  "It is not for the sake of the husband that the
  husband is dear, but for the sake of the Self
  that the husband is dear."

  "It is not for the sake of the wife that the wife
  is dear, but for the sake of the Self that the
  wife is dear."

  "It is not for the sake of the children, wealth,
  gods, beings, or the All that these are dear —
  but for the sake of the Self that all these
  are dear."

  "The Self should be seen, heard, reflected on,
  and contemplated, Maitreyi. By seeing, hearing,
  reflecting on, and contemplating the Self,
  all this is known."

This is the framework's Stage 2 (genuine source
already present) delivered directly: every authored-
landscape relationship, attachment, and investment
that the navigator takes to be their reason for
living is, at its root, the Self being sought
through the authored landscape's objects. The
navigator is not wrong to love their husband,
wife, children. But they are misidentifying the
source of the love's significance. The significance
of every authored-landscape relationship is that
the genuine coherence source is being sought
through it — and what is sought IS the navigator's
own genuine identity.

This is the most precise pre-modern statement of
the framework's claim about why the false attractor
basin has such powerful pull: it is the genuine
coherence source being sought through the authored
landscape's materials. The pull is real. The
direction is misidentified.

Yajnavalkya then delivers Neti Neti as the formal
conclusion: "After death there is no consciousness..."
— meaning, after the authored landscape's
identity constructs dissolve, the navigator is
not absent. They are not unconscious. They are
what was always present when those constructs
were stripped away.

Maitreyi's response: "Here you have bewildered me,
sir. I do not understand."

Yajnavalkya: "I am not saying anything bewildering.
This Self is indestructible and undying. Where
there is a duality of any kind, then one sees the
other, one hears the other, one knows the other.
But when everything has become the Self — whom
should one see by means of what? Whom should
one know by means of what? By means of what should
one know the knower?"

This is the koan structure in formal Sanskrit:
the authored landscape's knowing structure

knowing structure cannot apply itself to the
knower. What remains is not nothing. It is
what was always doing the knowing.

The dialogue ends without Maitreyi having received
the instrument — exactly as Nicodemus did not
receive it from Jesus. But the record serves the
distribution function: the reader who is ready
can receive what Maitreyi was not yet ready to receive.
