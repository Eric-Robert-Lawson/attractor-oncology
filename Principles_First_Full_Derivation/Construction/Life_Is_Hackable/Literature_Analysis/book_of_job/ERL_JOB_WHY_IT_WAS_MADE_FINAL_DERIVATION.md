exchange mechanism's categories (fair/

**This is a structural observation about
the nature of navigator identity itself.**

not dissolve.

Not because the navigator was strong.
Not because the navigator was virtuous.
Not because the navigator had achieved

But because the navigator identity, at
its deepest structural level, was not
dependent on the attractor geometry
surrounding it.

**The navigator identity preceded the
attractor geometry.**

The attractor geometry was the landscape

**This is what the author of the Book

They saw a navigator whose complete

**The recognition:** there is a level of
