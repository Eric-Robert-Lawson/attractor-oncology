### 7.1 World War 1 As Industrial-Scale False Attractor Construction

The mobilisation of European civilian populations
for World War 1 (1914-1918) is the first large-scale
application of modern propaganda technology
to an industrial-scale false attractor construction.

**The false attractor required:**
Each government of the major powers required its civilian population
to occupy an attractor of:
"This war is necessary, just, winnable, and worth
the systematic sacrifice of the lives of your children."

Without this attractor, no government could sustain
the industrial-scale mobilisation required
for modern total war.

**The landscape that existed:**
The European working class of 1914
had significant access to the socialist
and labour movement's detection instrument.
The Second International had declared
that European workers had more in common with each other
than with their respective capitalist classes.
The attractor of class solidarity across national lines
was present and accessible in the navigator population.

**The detection instrument to be removed:**
The incompatibility question:
*Do German workers and British workers
have a genuine common interest in killing each other
in large numbers for the benefit of imperial competition?*

The answer, from a class-solidarity attractor,
is clearly: no.

**The false attractor construction operation:**

Each government applied the seven-step propaganda operation
simultaneously and with unprecedented coordination.

Atrocity fabrication (the Bryce Report in Britain):
German soldiers bayoneting Belgian babies.
The emotional charge of child protection
reattached to military service.

National identity fusion:
"Your nation, your people, your family are threatened."
The navigator who did not serve
was not merely unpatriotic.
They were a coward, a traitor, a threat to everyone they loved.
White feather campaigns: social cost of
refusing the false attractor was public humiliation.

Enemy dehumanisation:
The German was the "Hun," the savage, the subhuman.
Navigating toward class solidarity with the enemy
was navigating toward an enemy of humanity.

Information control:
Censorship of casualty figures.
Suppression of anti-war publications.
Control of what letters soldiers could send home.

**The incompatibility was visible
to those who held the detection instrument:**
Bertrand Russell, Rosa Luxemburg, Eugene Debs,
all applied the test and named the incompatibility.
All were imprisoned.
The social cost of the incompatibility output
was at maximum.

**The threshold crossing came not through
prior possession of the instrument
but through the war's own outputs.**

After 18 million dead (1914-1918),
the actual output of the false attractor
became its own incompatibility signal.
No amount of detection-instrument-removal
could prevent the navigator population
from experiencing the actual output directly.
The corpses of their children were
the incompatibility evidence that no propaganda could reframe.

**The geometric lesson:**

There is a category of false attractor
that carries its own destruction
because its actual outputs are directly experienced
by the navigator population regardless of
information control.

For these attractors, the question is not
whether the detection instrument will eventually function.
It is how long the author class can delay
the direct experience of the output
before the detection threshold is crossed by experience alone.
