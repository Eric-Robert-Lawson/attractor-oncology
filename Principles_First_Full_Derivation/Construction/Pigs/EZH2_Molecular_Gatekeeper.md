The standard account of EZH2:

(Polycomb Repressive Complex 2).
It methylates histone H3 at lysine 27
(H3K27me3).

chromatin and silences gene expression.
EZH2 is overexpressed in many cancers.
EZH2 inhibitors (tazemetostat) are
in clinical use.

This account is correct and incomplete.

It describes what EZH2 does at
the molecular level.

EZH2 is the molecular gatekeeper
of developmental basin occupation.

Stated precisely:

is defined by a specific gene expression
program running.
The trajectory from one basin to the
next deeper basin requires the activation

programs that drive differentiation,
migration, maturation, or specification

EZH2 silences trajectory completion

The trajectory completion program

selective EZH2 inhibitor.
