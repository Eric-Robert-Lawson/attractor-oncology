Completion is a precise term.

It does not mean improvement.
It does not mean extension.

when every statement that is true within it

## Part I: What Bitcoin Proved

### The Theorem

On January 3, 2009,
Satoshi Nakamoto instantiated a proof.

Not a product. Not a company. Not a currency.

**A proof.**

The proof demonstrated that
a specific set of properties —
previously believed to be mutually exclusive —
could coexist in a single system:

```
PROPERTY 1 — FIXED SUPPLY:
  A monetary system can have an absolutely
  fixed supply that no authority can change.
  Previously: impossible without physical constraint
              (gold, silver, scarce commodities)
  Bitcoin proved: achievable through cryptographic
                  consensus without physical form.

PROPERTY 2 — TRUSTLESS TRANSFER:
  Value can be transferred between any two parties
  without the permission or participation
  of any trusted intermediary.
  Previously: impossible at global scale —
              all transfer required intermediaries
              (banks, clearinghouses, payment processors)
  Bitcoin proved: achievable through
                  distributed consensus.

PROPERTY 3 — NON-CUSTODIAL OWNERSHIP:
  Ownership of a monetary asset can be determined
  solely by cryptographic proof —
  requiring no institutional recognition,
  no legal system enforcement,
  no custodian's permission.
  Previously: impossible —
              all monetary ownership required
              institutional recognition
  Bitcoin proved: achievable through
                  cryptographic key control.

PROPERTY 4 — PUBLIC AUDITABILITY:
  The complete history of all monetary transfers
  can be publicly verifiable by any participant
  without compromising the privacy of
  the cryptographic ownership layer.
  Previously: impossible —
              audit required institutional access
  Bitcoin proved: achievable through
                  the public distributed ledger.

PROPERTY 5 — PROTOCOL IMMUTABILITY:
  The rules of a monetary system can be
  effectively permanent —
  resistant to change by any single authority
  including those who created the system.
  Previously: impossible —
              all monetary rules were changeable
              by the issuing authority
  Bitcoin proved: achievable through
                  distributed consensus with
                  aligned economic incentives.
```

**This is the theorem Bitcoin proved.**

Five properties. Previously believed mutually exclusive.
Demonstrated to coexist.
Demonstrated to be stable.
Demonstrated to be permanent.

The proof has been running continuously
for seventeen years.
No counterexample has been found.
The theorem holds.

---

### What the Theorem Did Not Prove

Bitcoin's theorem is precise.
Its precision defines its boundary.

The theorem proves that these five properties
can coexist in a transfer system.

**The theorem says nothing about custodial systems.**

Not because custodial systems are outside Bitcoin's scope —
but because custodial exchange requires
properties that transfer does not.

Transfer is atomic.
One party sends. Another receives.
The transfer is complete.
No ongoing state exists after settlement.

Custodial exchange is relational.
Capital exists under defined conditions.
Obligations persist over time.
State evolves as conditions are met or not met.
Multiple parties coordinate toward resolution.
Trust must be assessed before agreement.
Disputes must be resolved after disagreement.

**The five properties Bitcoin proved
are necessary but not sufficient
for custodial exchange.**

Bitcoin proved trustless transfer is possible.
Bitcoin did not prove trustless custodial exchange is possible.

That proof remained unwritten.
