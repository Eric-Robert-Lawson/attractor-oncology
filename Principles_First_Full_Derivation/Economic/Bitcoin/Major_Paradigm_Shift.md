### The Two Participant Types

```
Once Bitcoin is understood as a trading protocol network
rather than an asset, a structural distinction becomes clear:

  TYPE 1 — NETWORK USERS:
    Participants who engage with the network to transact.
    They use the protocol.
    They do not hold the protocol as a reserve.
    Their relationship to Bitcoin is functional and temporary.

  TYPE 2 — NETWORK OWNERS:
    Participants who hold Bitcoin as an ownership stake
    in the trading protocol network itself.
    Not to transact. Not to speculate.
    To RESERVE access to a network that is decoupled
    from predatory custodians.

  Network ownership is not investment.
  Investment implies:
    — Capital is deployed to generate return
    — Return is realized through sale

  Network ownership implies:
    — A stake is acquired in a protocol whose
      structural properties are the goal
    — The holding is permanent by design
    — Sale is not the mechanism of value extraction
    — Appreciation against reference currency is
      a secondary consequence, not the primary goal

  The value is in the stake entitling you to participate
  in a wealth reservation system that no predatory
  custodian can debase.

WEALTH SOVEREIGNTY:
  This is the primary utility.
  Not price appreciation.

  The ability to hold wealth in a protocol that
  cannot be debased, seized, inflated, or redirected
  by a predatory custodian.

  Before Bitcoin: gold served this function partially.
    — Gold cannot be debased (hard supply)

    — But gold is physical (seizeable)
    — But gold is not programmable or transmissible
      at the speed of the protocol

  Bitcoin inherits all of gold's wealth sovereignty
  properties and solves all of gold's failure modes.
  This is not analogous to gold. It is structurally
  superior to gold in every dimension relevant to
  wealth sovereignty.
