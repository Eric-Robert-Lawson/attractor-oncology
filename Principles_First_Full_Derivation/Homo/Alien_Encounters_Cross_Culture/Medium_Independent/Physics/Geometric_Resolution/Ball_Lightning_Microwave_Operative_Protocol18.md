**Procedure:**

**Step 1:** Take one whole grape. Cut in half across
the equator. Do not fully separate — leave a thin

et al. PNAS 2019.

**Step 2:** Place the grape dimer at the antinode

  (a) Slow the dispersal of ionised vapour

  — Slow-motion mode: minimum 240fps.
  — Frame the grape and the space above it.
  — Start recording BEFORE turning on the microwave.

**Step 5:** Set the microwave to FULL POWER.

**Step 6:** Record in the notebook:
  — Did plasma form? Y/N
  — Location of plasma relative to grape (gap vs surface)

    vs. brief persistence)

**Step 7:** Review slow-motion footage.
