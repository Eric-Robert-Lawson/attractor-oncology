## PART II — WHAT SLEPKOV 2019 ESTABLISHED
## AND WHAT IT LEFT OPEN

The Slepkov et al. PNAS 2019 paper:
"Plasma phenomena from microwave heating of grapes
and grape-sized water-rich spheres"
Tufts University / University of Guelph

**What it established [CONFIRMED]:**

1. The grape dimer acts as a coupled dielectric
   resonator at 2.45 GHz. The two halves behave
   as a plasmonic dimer antenna. The resonant mode
   concentrates the microwave electric field
   specifically at the contact gap between the
   two halves — not at the grape surfaces.

2. A single grape does NOT produce plasma.
   Two grapes in contact DO.
   The plasma forms at the gap, not at either sphere.
   This is the dimer resonance mechanism.

3. The effect works with any grape-sized water-rich
   sphere. It is NOT biology-specific. It is
   geometry-specific. The grape is an accidental
   match to the resonant dimer geometry at 2.45 GHz.

4. The plasma emission spectrum contains potassium
   lines (766nm, 770nm) and sodium lines (589nm)
   from the grape mineral content. These are
   LINE emissions, not continuous blackbody spectrum.

5. The plasma is an ionic plasma — ions from the
   grape electrolyte (K+, Na+) are driven into the
   vapor phase and excited to emission.

**What it left open [UNKNOWN to Slepkov]:**

1. Why the plasma sphere sometimes hovers ABOVE the
   grape and persists after the initial arc.
   Slepkov described the mechanism of initiation.
   He did not describe the mechanism of persistence.

2. Why the sphere is spherical rather than arc-like
   or filamentary in the persistence phase.
   The dimer geometry produces a localised antinode.
   It does not explain why a free-floating sphere
   forms and maintains spherical shape.

3. What determines the sphere's lifetime.
   Slepkov noted the phenomenon. He did not derive
   a lifetime from any physical principle.

4. Whether the sphere is a driven plasma
   (exists only while microwave is on) or
   an autocatalytic plasma (partially self-sustaining).
   Slepkov did not test this distinction.

These four open items are exactly Protocol V2
Phases 1, 2, 4, and the PO-2/PO-4 predictions.
Slepkov 2019 is Protocol V2 Phase 1 run without
Phases 2 through 8.
