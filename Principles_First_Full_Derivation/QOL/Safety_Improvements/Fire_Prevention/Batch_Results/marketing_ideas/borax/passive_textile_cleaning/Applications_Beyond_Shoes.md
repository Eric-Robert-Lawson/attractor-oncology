## PART I: THE CORE GEOMETRY — STATED PRECISELY

### 1.1 — Why This Works in Any Enclosed Space

```
The shoe packet works because of
three structural conditions:

CONDITION 1 — ENCLOSED VOLUME:
  The space has defined boundaries.
  Air within the space is not
  continuously replaced by
  large-volume fresh airflow.
  The same air recirculates
  across the packet surface
  repeatedly.
  Each pass: more VOCs adsorbed.
  Each pass: more microbes suppressed.
  Each pass: more acid odour compounds
  neutralised.

CONDITION 2 — ODOUR SOURCE PRESENT:
  The space contains either:
    A biological source
    (bacteria, mold, fungal growth)
    producing ongoing VOC emissions.
  OR:
    Absorbed/embedded odour compounds
    in porous surfaces
    (fabric, foam, wood, rubber)
    off-gassing into the space.
  OR both.

CONDITION 3 — PACKET ACCESSIBLE TO AIR:
  The perforated packet must be
  positioned such that air in
  the space contacts the gel surface.
  Gel surface area exposed to air
  is the rate-limiting variable.
  More holes = more exposure = faster
  treatment but shorter duration.
  Fewer holes = slower treatment
  but longer sustained action.

IF ALL THREE CONDITIONS PRESENT:
  The packet treats the space.
  Not metaphorically.
  Mechanistically.
  The same three chemistry pathways
  operate regardless of what the
  enclosure is made of or what
  it is used for.

THE ONLY VARIABLES THAT CHANGE:
  Volume of the enclosed space.
    → Determines number of packets required.
  Intensity of odour source.
    → Determines treatment duration.
  Air circulation within the space.
    → Determines contact rate with packet.
  Geometry of the space.
    → Determines packet placement strategy.

The chemistry does not change.
The mechanism does not change.
The packet does not change.
Only the deployment geometry changes.
```

### 1.2 — The Sizing Principle

```
The shoe contains approximately
500–800mL of enclosed air volume.
One 200mL packet treats it effectively.

DERIVED SIZING RATIO:
  1 × 200mL packet per 500–800mL
  enclosed air volume.

  This ratio is a starting approximation.
  It will vary with:
    Odour source intensity.
    Air circulation rate.
    Surface porosity of the enclosure.

  For odour-saturated spaces:
  increase packet count.
  For lightly contaminated spaces:
  reduce packet count or use
  smaller packet size.

SCALING:
  Small enclosed space (~5L): 1 packet.
  Medium enclosed space (~20L): 3–4 packets.
  Large enclosed space (~100L): 15–20 packets.
  Room-scale (~10,000L): not suited to
  this passive mechanism alone —
  see Part IV for the active variant.
```

## PART IV: THE ACTIVE VARIANT — ROOM SCALE

```
The passive packet mechanism
has a volume ceiling.

Above approximately 200L enclosed space,
passive diffusion from a fixed packet
becomes rate-limited:
  VOCs emitted from surfaces faster
  than packet can adsorb from
  slowly diffusing air.
  Treatment time extends to weeks.
  Effectiveness per unit volume drops.

The geometry that breaks this ceiling:

ACTIVE CIRCULATION VARIANT:

  A small fan (USB-powered, low flow)
  draws room air across a P1S-B
  gel surface continuously.

  The fan is the pump.
  The gel surface is the treatment medium.
  The room air is the flow.

  This is the same geometry as
  the active thermal suit
  (pump + gel + membrane)
  applied to air treatment instead of
  thermal protection.

  The fixed point:
    Air treatment capacity =
    f(gel surface area × air flow rate
    × adsorption capacity per unit area).

  ROOM-SCALE PRODUCT CONCEPT:
    Open tray or mesh container
    filled with P1S-B gel.
    Small USB fan positioned to
    draw room air across gel surface.
    Air passes through gel surface layer,
    VOCs adsorbed by bentonite,
    microbes suppressed by borax vapour,
    acid compounds neutralised by bicarb.
    Treated air exits back into room.

  This is a mineral-chemistry
  air purifier.
  No HEPA filter.
  No activated carbon cartridge.
  No ozone generation.
  No UV lamp.
  One gel tray. One small fan.
  Replaceable gel tray every 4–8 weeks.

  The market this enters:
    Air purifier market: $12B globally.
    Dominated by HEPA + activated
    carbon filter systems.
    P1S-B active variant:
      Lower cost per unit.
      Refillable (gel tray replacement).
      No filter shipping required.
      Natural mineral chemistry
      (market differentiator).
      Antimicrobial + adsorption +
      neutralisation simultaneously.
```

## PART VI: FALSIFICATION CONDITIONS

```
The enclosed-space generalization holds if:

F1 — MECHANISM TRANSFER:
  The same three-mechanism chemistry
  that reduces shoe odour 80–95%
  produces measurable odour reduction
  in at least 3 other enclosed space
  categories under controlled testing.

  FALSIFIED IF:
  Shoe result is specific to shoe
  geometry or shoe odour chemistry
  and does not replicate in
  structurally different enclosures.

F2 — SCALING LINEARITY:
  Increasing packet count proportionally
  to enclosed volume maintains
  treatment effectiveness.

  FALSIFIED IF:
  Passive diffusion limit makes
  scaling non-linear above a
  threshold volume — i.e., 10×
  the packets does not produce
  equivalent treatment in 10×
  the volume.
  (This is likely true above ~50L —
  active variant required at room scale.)

F3 — SHELF LIFE ACROSS FORMATS:
  The 12–18 month unrefrigerated
  shelf life demonstrated in
  sealed ziplock format holds
  across all packaging variants
  including mesh bag (fridge variant)
  and larger commercial formats.

  FALSIFIED IF:
  Mesh bag format accelerates
  evaporation or contamination
  and reduces shelf life below
  claimed window.
  (Likely true — open formats will
  have shorter shelf life.
  This requires per-format testing.)

F4 — CONSUMER RESIDUE TOLERANCE:
  Across all deployment contexts,
  gel residue after treatment
  is manageable by consumer
  without damage to surfaces.

  FALSIFIED IF:
  Bentonite clay residue in
  vehicle upholstery, closet fabrics,
  or other surfaces causes staining
  or damage requiring remediation.
```
