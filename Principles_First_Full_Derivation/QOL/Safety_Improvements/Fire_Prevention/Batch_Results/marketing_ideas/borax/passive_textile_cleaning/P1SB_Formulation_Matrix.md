# 2. IMPORTANT FORMULATION PRINCIPLES

---

# 2.1 — SAP OVERLOAD FAILURE

Too much SAP causes:
- excessive gel rigidity
- poor textile penetration
- rinse difficulty
- residue retention

Thus:
# passive textile formulations require LOWER SAP concentrations than thermal formulations.

---

# 2.2 — BENTONITE OVERLOAD FAILURE

Too much bentonite causes:
- clay packing into fibers
- excessive residue
- rinse burden
- fabric stiffness

Thus:
# bentonite should remain moderate unless particulate adsorption is primary goal.

---

# 2.3 — BORAX LIMITATIONS

Borax advantages:
- mild alkalinity
- deodorization
- microbial suppression
- oil destabilization

But excessive borax:
- may dry fibers
- may affect dyes
- may increase residue
- may stiffen textiles

Thus:
# textile-safe concentration windows matter.
