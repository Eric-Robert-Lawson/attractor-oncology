- Quick recontamination

## SHOE-SPECIFIC FORMULATION

### Formulation for Shoe Odor Treatment

**For:** Athletic shoes, casual shoes, work boots, any smelly footwear
**Duration:** 4-8 hours (overnight is practical)
**Batch size:** 1500mL (sufficient for 2-3 shoes)

```
SHOE ODOR TREATMENT FORMULATION

INGREDIENTS (per 1500mL batch):
  Distilled water ............ 1500mL
  SAP powder ................ 8g
  Bentonite clay ............ 28g
  Borax powder .............. 18g
  Sodium bicarbonate ........ 10g (odor neutralization)
  Tea tree oil .............. 3-5 drops (optional, antimicrobial boost)

Total dry: 56g
Total volume: ~1510mL

PROPERTIES:
  - Medium-high viscosity
  - Strong alkalinity (pH 8.5-9.2)
  - High odor adsorption capacity
  - Antimicrobial (borax + optional tea tree)
  - Drying assistance (hygroscopic)

PREP PROTOCOL (standard):
  1. Measure 1500mL distilled water
  2. Add 10g sodium bicarbonate, stir to dissolve
  3. Optional: add 3-5 drops tea tree oil, stir
  4. Dry mix: 8g SAP + 28g bentonite + 18g borax
  5. Stir dry blend 90 seconds
  6. Set up immersion blender, high speed, create vortex
  7. Add dry mix slowly at liquid surface over 90 seconds
  8. Blend continuously 10 minutes at high speed
  9. Cover with plastic wrap, rest 60 minutes
  10. Ready to use

APPLICATION PROTOCOL:
  1. Place shoes in container (wide bowl or basin works)
  2. Pour gel directly into shoes (insoles and linings)
  3. Optional: massage gel into fabric/padding gently
  4. Submerge shoes in remaining gel if possible
     (shoes sitting in gel pool is better than gel-only)
  5. Cover container with lid or plastic wrap
  6. Leave undisturbed 4-8 hours (overnight ideal)
  7. Remove shoes, drain excess gel into sink
  8. Rinse shoes under running water (gentle spray, 2-3 minutes)
  9. Wring out excess water gently
  10. Stuff with newspaper (change after 2-4 hours)
  11. Air dry at room temperature (12-24 hours)

EXPECTED RESULT:
  - Odor: 80-95% reduction (nearly completely gone)
  - Freshness: lasts 2-4 weeks normal wear
  - Moisture: reduced (SAP accelerates drying)
  - Bacterial biofilm: suppressed by borax
  - Fungal risk: reduced
```
