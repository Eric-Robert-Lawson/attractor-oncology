```

  Daily harvest mass: 960g + 3,201g = 4,161g.
  = 4.16kg/day.
  Annual harvest: 4.16 × 365 = 1,518.4kg.
  = 1,518kg/year per cow.

  Per day: 3,201g × $0.000385/g = $1.232.
  Per year: $1.232 × 365 = $449.68/year.
  Rounding: ~$450/year input cost.

WASTE MANAGEMENT COST ELIMINATED:

  Current manure management:
  $130–$280/year.

  from a waste product to a dry solid.

  (urine is the liquid fraction that
  drives lagoon management, ammonia

  ~$60–$150/year per cow.

    1,518 × $10 = $15,180/year.
```
