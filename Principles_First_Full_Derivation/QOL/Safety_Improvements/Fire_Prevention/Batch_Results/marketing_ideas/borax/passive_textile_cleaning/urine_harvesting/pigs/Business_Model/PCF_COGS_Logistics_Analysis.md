### 1.3 — Slurry Quality & Procurement Agreement

**Quality Specifications:**

```
What You Need from Slurry:

Characteristics:
- Liquid consistency (not solid manure)
- 3-7% solids content (typical liquid slurry)
- Free of antibiotics/growth promoters (if possible)
- No visible contamination (plastic, feed, dirt)
- pH 6.0-8.0 (neutral, not acidic)
- Temperature acceptable (fresh, not weeks old)

How to Verify:
1. Visual inspection on pickup
   - Smell: Should be pork farm smell, not rotten
   - Color: Brown/tan, not black or green
   - Consistency: Pourable, not thick sludge

2. Simple tests (no lab required)
   - pH test: $5 pH meter from hardware store
   - Solids test: Pour through fine mesh, see separation
   - Age test: Ask farm "When was this batch pumped?"

3. Lab testing (if concerned)
   - Send 1L sample to ag lab ($50)
   - Check for contaminants, heavy metals
   - Do this once per farm annually

Simple Procurement Agreement (1-page):

2. Schedule: Monday mornings, delivered to OrganismCore facility

4. Delivery: Farm is responsible for transportation

7. Storage: Farm acknowledges OrganismCore has proper containment

Signed: _________________ Date: _________
