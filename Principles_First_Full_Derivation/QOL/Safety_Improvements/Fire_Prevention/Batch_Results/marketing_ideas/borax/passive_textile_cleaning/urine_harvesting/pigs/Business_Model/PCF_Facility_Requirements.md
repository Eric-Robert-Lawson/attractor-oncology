### 1.1 — Critical Requirements (Deal-Breakers)

```
These are NON-NEGOTIABLE. If your dad's shop doesn't have these,
the operation won't work.

REQUIREMENT 1: CEILING HEIGHT ≥ 13 FEET
════════════════════════════════════════

Why needed: Flash dryer (XSG-2) is vertical, requires 13 feet
            to fit properly plus 1-2 feet for access/maintenance

Minimum: 13 feet clear floor to ceiling (no beams/obstructions)
Ideal: 14-16 feet (gives working room above dryer)

How to measure:
1. Stand inside facility with measuring tape
2. Measure from floor to lowest ceiling point (check for beams)
3. Document height in different locations (ceiling may be uneven)

CHECK: Go measure your dad's shop now. What's the ceiling height?
Typical garage: 10-12 feet (MARGINAL - might work with creative placement)
Commercial shop: 12-16 feet (IDEAL)
Industrial facility: 14-20+ feet (PERFECT)

If your dad's shop is auto repair garage: Ceiling is typically 12 feet
Status: MARGINAL (can work, but tight)

If your dad's shop is warehouse/manufacturing space: Ceiling is 14-18 feet
Status: EXCELLENT ✓

⚠️ CRITICAL: What is the ceiling height in your dad's shop?
This is the #1 determining factor.

REQUIREMENT 2: ELECTRICAL SERVICE ≥ 200 AMP @ 220V
══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Why needed: Flash dryer and pump require 220V power
            Multiple circuits needed simultaneously

Current service needed:
- Main panel: 200+ amp service (not individual circuit amperage)
- Voltage: 220V (or 240V, same thing)
- Phase: 3-phase preferred (but single-phase can work with limitations)

Typical commercial garage: Yes, has 200 amp service
Typical residential garage: No, usually 100-150 amps (NOT sufficient)

How to check:
1. Look at electrical panel in facility
2. Note the main breaker amperage (look at main disconnect)
3. Check if it says "200", "150", or "100"
4. Look for 220V outlet (different shape than 110V)

Status: If dad's shop has commercial power: ✓ You're good
Status: If it's residential garage: ✗ May need upgrade ($1-3K)

⚠️ CRITICAL: Ask your dad: "What's your main electrical service?"
Expected answer: "200 amp service" or "I have 3-phase power"

REQUIREMENT 3: WATER SUPPLY & FLOOR DRAIN
══════════════════════════════════════════

Why needed: Equipment flushing (10L/day)
           Cleaning water runoff

Minimum: Hose connection + floor drain within 50 feet
Ideal: Hose bib and drain in production area

How to check:
1. Is there a hose bibb (outdoor water connection) on the facility?
2. Is there a floor drain visible (looks like a sink in floor)?
3. Are these located reasonably close to where equipment will be?

Typical commercial shop: Yes, has both
Typical residential garage: May or may not have

Status: If your dad's shop has these: ✓ You're good
Status: If not: Cost to add = $200-400 (very manageable)

⚠️ MODERATE: Ask your dad: "Is there water/drain access near the shop?"

REQUIREMENT 4: GAS LINE (NATURAL GAS)
═════════════════════════════════════

Why needed: Heat for flash dryer (required)

Minimum: Natural gas available within 30 feet of equipment
Ideal: Gas line already running through facility

How to check:
1. Do you smell gas in the facility? (Natural gas has odor added)
2. Are there gas appliances (furnace, water heater) in/near facility?
3. Ask your dad: "Do you have natural gas to the shop?"

Typical commercial shop: Often has gas (if used for heating/hot water)
Typical garage: May or may not have

Status: If gas available: ✓ You're good
Status: If not: Cost to add = $300-600 (manageable)
       Note: May require gas company to install (can't DIY)

⚠️ CRITICAL: Ask your dad: "Is natural gas available to the shop?"
Expected answer: "Yes, we have gas" (most commercial shops do)
