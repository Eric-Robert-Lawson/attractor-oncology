## PART II: REVISED PROTOCOL - PRAGMATIC 8-HOUR EXECUTION

### 2.1 — MORNING PREP (30-45 minutes, 6:00-6:45am)

**Goal:** Have equipment ready, all materials staged, before production begins

**Step 1: Pre-Blend Borax into Bentonite Supply (DONE ONCE PER WEEK)**

```
WEEKLY PREPARATION (Every Monday morning):
Not daily - this is the KEY OPTIMIZATION

Weekly borax blending (instead of batch-by-batch):
─────────────────────────────────────────────────

Weekly Borax Dose:        39g borax × 5 days = 195g borax for week
Weekly Bentonite Amount:  10.5kg/day × 5 days = 52.5kg bentonite

Blending Procedure:
1. Place 52.5kg bentonite in large 50-liter plastic container
2. Measure 195g borax on digital scale (±1g precision)
3. Pour borax into 1-liter bottle with 0.5L bentonite
4. Shake bottle vigorously for 5 minutes (borax distributes into clay)
5. Pour bentonite+borax mixture back into main 50-liter container
6. Stir entire 50-liter container for 10 minutes with large paddle
7. Label: "BORAX-BLENDED BENTONITE - WEEK OF [DATE]"
8. Cover container, store in cool location
9. This is now your "pre-blended clay" for entire week
10. Daily routine: Just scoop 10.5kg from this container

Result: 
- Borax uniformly distributed throughout entire weekly bentonite supply
- Daily operation: ZERO manual borax handling
- Safety: Borax uniformly distributed, no hotspots possible
- Time saved: 2 hours/week vs. 30 minutes/day of manual pre-mixing
```

### 2.3 — CLEANUP & SHUTDOWN (3:00pm - 3:30pm, 30 minutes)

**Goal:** Clean equipment, secure systems, prepare for next day

**Step 1: Stop Production Sequence (3:00pm Exactly)**

```
Shutdown Order (CRITICAL - must be in this sequence):

1. STOP DRY AUGER
   - Flip dry feeder auger OFF
   - Hopper level will drop as pump continues flowing

2. WAIT 2 MINUTES
   - This empties the inline mixer of remaining slurry
   - Any dry mix left in pipe flows into dryer
   - No caking occurs

3. STOP PUMP
   - Flip pump OFF on VFD
   - Vortex in dryer continues briefly (coasting down)
   - Last batch completes drying

4. WAIT 1 MINUTE
   - Dryer vortex decelerates naturally
   - No sudden shock to equipment

5. SHUT OFF GAS HEATER
   - Flip gas heater OFF
   - Temperature begins dropping
   - Dryer will cool from 140°C to ~50°C over 30-40 minutes

6. KEEP BLOWER FAN ON for 5 minutes
   - Fan pushes hot air through carbon filter
   - Prevents moisture condensation in filter bed
   - Then turn blower OFF

Result: System is now safely depressurizing and cooling
```

**Step 2: Flush Inline Static Mixer (3:10pm)**

```
Purpose: Remove slurry residue from mixer, prevent caking

Equipment: Clean water source (or deionized water)
Time: 10 minutes

Procedure:
1. Attach clean water line to pump intake (or manually pour water into mixer via funnel)
2. Run pump for 2-3 minutes
   - Water flows through mixer and into dryer
   - Flushes out slurry residue
   - Moisture vaporizes in 140°C chamber (normal)
3. Continue flushing until outlet water runs clear (3-5 minutes)
4. Stop pump
5. Disconnect water line
6. Mixer is now clean (no caking overnight)

Why this matters:
- Slurry is 256.95g per batch
- Builds up in mixer over 8 hours
- Overnight, becomes a hard cake (ruins equipment)
- 10-minute flush prevents this entirely
```
