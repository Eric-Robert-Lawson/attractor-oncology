### 2.1 — WEEKLY PREPARATION (Every Monday Morning, 15 minutes)

**Goal:** Pre-blend borax into bentonite supply for entire week, eliminating daily bottleneck

**Time:** 6:00am - 6:15am (BEFORE regular production starts)

**Equipment Needed:**
- One 50-liter plastic container with lid
- Digital scale (±1g precision minimum)
- Large paddle or hand mixer
- 1-liter plastic bottle with cap

**Weekly Borax Pre-Blend Procedure:**

```
STEP 1: Measurement (2 minutes)
─────────────────────────────

1. Calculate weekly borax requirement:
   Daily borax: 39g
   Days of production: 5 days/week (Mon-Fri)
   Weekly total: 39g × 5 days = 195g borax

2. Measure on digital scale:
   Place 1-liter bottle on scale
   Zero scale
   Add borax slowly until scale reads 195g ±1g
   Cap bottle, set aside

3. Measure bentonite:
   Place large 50-liter container on scale
   Pre-weighed bentonite from supplier (or measure)
   Total needed: 10.5kg × 5 days = 52.5kg bentonite
   Pour into 50-liter container

STEP 2: Initial Mixing (5 minutes)
──────────────────────────────────

1. Pour 0.5 liters of bentonite into 1-liter bottle containing 195g borax
2. Cap bottle
3. Shake vigorously for 2 minutes
   - Borax particles distribute through clay
   - No clumping (vigorous shaking prevents this)
4. Pour bentonite+borax mixture back into main 50-liter container

STEP 3: Complete Blending (5 minutes)
─────────────────────────────────────

1. Using large paddle, stir entire 50-liter container
2. Mix for 10 full minutes
   - Large circular motions
   - Bottom to top (ensure all bentonite touches paddle)
   - Edge to center (distribute throughout)
   - Mix until uniform color (no white spots of borax visible)

3. Visual verification:
   - Sample handful of bentonite from different spots
   - All should look identical (uniform brown)
   - No visible borax crystals
   - Consistent appearance throughout

STEP 4: Storage & Labeling (1 minute)
─────────────────────────────────────

1. Cover 50-liter container with lid
   - Prevents moisture absorption during week
   - Keeps bentonite fresh

2. Label clearly:
   "BORAX-BLENDED BENTONITE - WEEK OF [DATE]"
   "CONTENTS: 52.5kg bentonite + 195g borax"
   "USE: 10.5kg per day Mon-Fri"

3. Store in cool, dry location
   - Preferably where you'll measure from daily
   - Away from direct sunlight
   - Away from moisture sources

VERIFICATION:
After mixing, take one 200g sample and send to lab optional (quarterly):
- Lab verifies: Boron content 0.1% throughout sample
- Confirms: Pre-blending is uniform and effective
- Cost: ~$200 (quarterly validation, not every week)

TOTAL TIME: 15 minutes per week
BENEFIT: Zero borax handling or pre-mixing during daily production
RESULT: Clean, efficient, eliminates bottleneck entirely
```

- Production can begin at 7:00am
- This gives 20-minute buffer (good practice)

- Check PID settings: Might be set to lower temperature
- Call equipment vendor: They can help troubleshoot

- Refer to equipment manual

### 2.4 — SHUTDOWN & CLEANUP PHASE (3:00pm - 3:30pm, 30 minutes)

**Goal:** Safely cool equipment, prevent caking, secure facility for next day

**Shutdown Sequence (CRITICAL - Must follow exact order)**

```

──────────────────────────────────────

Time: 1 second

- Auger stops first
- Pump continues for 2 more minutes
- Remaining slurry flows through mixer and dryer
- No powder fed means no mixture backing up
- Prevents clogging of mixer

Verification:
- Listen: Auger grinding sound stops
- Watch: Hopper level drops as pump continues
- Expected: Hopper empties for next 2 minutes

STEP 2: WAIT 2 MINUTES (3:00pm - 3:02pm)
─────────────────────────────────────────

Do nothing. Let pump continue.
- Slurry flows through inline mixer
- Flows into flash dryer
- Last batch of slurry being processed
- No powder to interfere = clean discharge

Purpose:
- Clears mixer of any powder residue
- Prevents caking when mixer cools
- Prevents powder-slurry sludge formation

Verification (at 3:02pm):
- Dryer still operating normally
- Temperature still 140°C
- Sound still steady
- Powder still collecting (final batches)

STEP 3: STOP PUMP (3:02pm)

## PART V: TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

### 5.1 — Common Issues & Solutions

```
PROBLEM 1: TEMPERATURE NOT REACHING 140°C
──────────────────────────────────────────

Symptoms:
- PID display shows temperature stuck <130°C
- After 20 minutes of heating, still rising slowly
- Production shouldn't start until 140°C achieved

Possible causes:
1. Gas line kinked or restricted
   - Solution: Check entire gas line path, ensure no kinks
   - Time to fix: 10 minutes
   
2. Gas valve not opening fully
   - Solution: Call PID programmer to verify valve signal
   - Time to fix: 30 minutes
   
3. Thermocouple (temperature sensor) misreading
   - Solution: Verify PID can read temperature correctly
   - Time to fix: 15 minutes (check connections)
   
4. Heater element failure
   - Solution: Call equipment vendor, replacement may be needed
   - Time to fix: Could be 1-2 days

If you can't fix quickly:
- Don't start production (will produce wet product)
- Call Aaron Equipment or your equipment vendor
- They can diagnose remotely or send technician
- Wait for fix before resuming production

PROBLEM 2: POWDER OUTPUT UNUSUALLY WET/CLUMPY
───────────────────────────────────────────────

Symptoms:
- Product exiting dryer is moist (not powder-dry)
- Clumping in pouches (not free-flowing)
- Failing moisture content check (>3%)

Possible causes:
1. Dryer temperature dropped <135°C
   - Check PID: Is it showing 140°C?
   - If not: Refer to Problem 1 above
   - Solution: Increase temperature to 140°C

2. Slurry feed rate too high
   - Vortex can't dry material fast enough
   - Material exits before fully dry
   - Solution: Check auger speed (might be set too fast)
   - Reduce auger RPM by 10% (slower feed = more dry time)

3. Air humidity unusually high
   - On very humid days (summer rain), air intake adds moisture
   - Solution: Ensure blower intake is from facility (not outdoor)
   - Check if intake filter is clogged (restricts air flow)

4. Inline mixer broken or clogged
   - If mixture not mixing properly, water doesn't distribute
   - Solution: Run flush procedure (see Section 2.4)
   - If still wet: Mixer may need replacement

Action plan:
1. Check temperature (highest probability issue)
2. If temperature is 140°C: Slow down auger speed
3. Run moisture test after fix
4. If still failing: Call equipment vendor

PROBLEM 3: BENTONITE DUST ESCAPING FROM BLENDER
─────────────────────────────────────────────────

Symptoms:
- Visible dust cloud during blending
- Dust layer on nearby surfaces
- Operator respiratory irritation

Possible causes:
1. Enclosure seal degraded (gap developed)
   - Vibration during mixing loosened bolts
   - Solution: Tighten all enclosure bolts
   - Time: 10 minutes
   
2. Ductwork disconnected
   - Flex duct came loose from blender
   - Solution: Reconnect and secure with clamp
   - Time: 5 minutes
   
3. Dust collector filter clogged
   - No suction (can't capture dust)
   - Solution: Empty or replace filter
   - Time: 10 minutes
   
4. Dust collector broken or disconnected
   - Motor not running or duct disconnected from collector
   - Solution: Verify connections, restart collector motor
   - Time: 5 minutes

Action plan:
1. PUT ON P100 RESPIRATOR IMMEDIATELY (safety first)
2. Stop blender operation
3. Check ductwork connections (visual)
4. Check dust collector filter (visual, check level)
5. Empty filter if >50% full
6. Restart blender, verify dust capture
7. If still escaping: Check enclosure bolts
8. If still escaping: Call vendor (possible enclosure damage)

Prevention:
- Monthly inspection of enclosure bolts
- Weekly check of ductwork connections
- Weekly check of dust collector filter level

PROBLEM 4: AUGER FILLER NOT DISPENSING ACCURATE AMOUNTS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Symptoms:
- Weekly weight check: Pouches are 0.95kg (underfilled)
- Or: Pouches are 1.05kg (overfilled)
- Inconsistent weights from day to day

Possible causes:
1. Auger outlet gate opening changed
   - Mechanical drift from vibration
   - Solution: Adjust gate opening (wider = more powder)
   - Time: 10 minutes

2. Powder density changed (moisture absorbed)
   - Bentonite absorbed moisture, now heavier per volume
   - Solution: Ensure bentonite stored in sealed container
   - Time: Ongoing (for next batches)

3. Auger speed changed
   - VFD speed drifted or button accidentally pressed
   - Solution: Verify VFD shows correct auger RPM
   - Time: 5 minutes

4. Hopper level too low
   - Auger can't get full volumetric dose
   - Solution: Keep hopper filled above minimum line
   - Time: Check daily

Action plan:
1. Weigh 5 random pouches
2. Calculate average weight
3. If underfilled: Widen auger outlet gate slightly
4. If overfilled: Narrow auger outlet gate slightly
5. Retest after adjustment
6. If still not accurate: Check powder density (store properly)

Prevention:
- Weekly weight verification (every Friday)
- Keep auger settings documented
- Store bentonite in sealed containers

PROBLEM 5: PUMP NOT DRAWING SLURRY
────────────────────────────────────

Symptoms:
- VFD started, but no slurry flowing to mixer
- Pump sounds like it's running, but no output
- Hopper is filling (auger works), but no liquid feed

Possible causes:
1. IBC tote valve is CLOSED
   - Solution: OPEN the valve (this is #1 cause!)
   - Location: Bottom-right of IBC tote
   - Time: 10 seconds

2. Pump inlet line kinked or blocked
   - Solution: Check entire intake line for kinks
   - Straighten if bent
   - Time: 5 minutes

3. Pump priming issue
   - Air in pump line prevents suction
   - Solution: Run pump backwards for 10 seconds (reverses suction)
   - Or: Manually pour water into pump inlet to prime
   - Time: 2 minutes

4. Pump cavitation (air leak in suction line)
   - Small air leak reduces suction
   - Solution: Check all inlet connections for tightness
   - Tighten any loose fittings
   - Time: 10 minutes

5. Pump failure
   - Pump isn't working (unlikely, but possible)
   - Solution: Call equipment vendor
   - Time: 1-2 hours for replacement

Action plan:
1. FIRST: Check IBC tote valve (is it OPEN?)
2. SECOND: Look at intake line (any kinks?)
3. THIRD: Listen to pump (is it making suction noise?)
4. If still not working: Check inlet connections
5. If still not working: Call vendor

Prevention:
- Every morning: Verify IBC tote valve is OPEN
- Weekly: Visually inspect intake line
- Document: Valve position in checklist
```
