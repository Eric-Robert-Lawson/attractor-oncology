- Customer sues for damages
- Insurance claim filed

```

- Regulatory action against customer (EPA investigation)

- Your product blamed for crop contamination

4. Document all testing

- "Premium slow-release fertilizer"
- "8-14-6 NPK formulation"
- "Lab-tested for consistency"
- "Optimized for cannabis cultivation"

These are all legal claims (factual, not exaggerated).

Status: AVOIDABLE by not making false claims

- Don't handle finished cannabis (ever)
- Don't position as part of cannabis business (just supplier)
- Don't advertise as "cannabis expert" (position as fertilizer maker)
