NEGOTIATING SLURRY SUPPLY AGREEMENT:
════════════════════════════════════

Key Terms to Negotiate:

Term 1: Volume & Frequency
- What you want: "X gallons per day, delivered [X] times per week"
- Example: "2,000 gallons 3x per week (Monday/Wednesday/Friday)"
- Flexibility: "Can vary ±20% based on demand"
- Why: Predictable supply for planning

Term 2: Delivery Method
- Option A: They deliver (you pay delivery fee)
- Option B: You pick up (you pay fuel, they provide access)
- Option C: Mix (they deliver, you pick up sometimes)
- Typical cost: $500-1,500/month in delivery
- Your budget: Can afford this from profit

Term 3: Pricing
- Option A: They pay YOU to take it (best case)
  * "We'll remove your waste free" (saves them disposal cost)
  * They may even pay you per ton
  * Typical: $0-10/ton (you're doing them a service)
  
- Option B: You pay them (worst case)
  * "We'll buy your slurry at $X per ton"
  * Not ideal, but sometimes necessary
  * Typical cost: $20-50/ton (cheap compared to disposal for them)
  
- Recommended: Pitch Option A
  * "We're removing your slurry (environmental solution)"
  * "You save $X/month disposal cost"
  * "Can we arrange pickup 3x/week?"

Term 3: Quality & Consistency
- What you need: Know what's in the slurry (composition)
- Question: "What are you feeding pigs? Any medications?"
- Why: Affects hydrogel quality
- Agreement: Notify you of major diet changes

Term 4: Contract Duration
- Recommended: 1-year minimum (with 30-day termination clause)
- Gives: Predictability for you
- Flexibility: Allows exit if relationship doesn't work
- Renewal: Auto-renew annually unless terminated

Term 5: Access & Liability
- Clarify: Who has liability if accident occurs during pickup?
- Recommended: Their insurance covers while at their facility
- Your insurance: Covers during transport to your facility
