## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY - BEFORE WE START

What you're doing is **literally just**:

1. **Receiving slurry** (waste collector pumps it into your tank)
2. **Pumping it out** (you transfer it to blender)
3. **Mixing it with powder** (dry ingredients go in, it blends)
4. **Packaging the result** (fills containers, seals them)
5. **Stacking the finished product** (ready to sell)

That's it. Five steps. No magic. No complex chemistry.

The operations are **not tight or impossible**. You have **hours of buffer time** every day.

Let me walk you through what you're literally doing, piece by piece, so you can see it's manageable.
