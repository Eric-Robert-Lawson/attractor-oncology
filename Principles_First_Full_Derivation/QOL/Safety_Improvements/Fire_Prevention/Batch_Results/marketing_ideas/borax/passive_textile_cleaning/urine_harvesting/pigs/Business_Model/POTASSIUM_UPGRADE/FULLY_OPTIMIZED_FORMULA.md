- Swelling: Fully swollen with water (gel state achieved)
- Breakdown rate: ~0.5-1% per week initially (slow degradation)
- Timeline:
  * Week 0-2: 0-2% degraded (gel fully functional)

  * Week 4-8: 4-8% degraded (still >92% functional)
  * Week 8-12: 12-20% degraded (beginning to lose effectiveness)
  * Week 12+: >20% degraded (gradual loss of gel properties)

Degradation mechanism:
- Microbial enzymes (protease-like) attack polymer chains
- pH 3.5-4 slightly accelerates degradation (vs. neutral pH)
- Water temperature affects rate (warmer = faster degradation)
- At 20°C: ~0.5-1% per week; at 25°C: ~1-1.5% per week

End products of degradation:
- Smaller polymer fragments (oligomers, still gel-like)
- Eventually: Acrylic acid monomers and other organic acids
- These become part of organic matter pool (can be further metabolized)
- Final degradation: Complete mineralization to CO₂ + H₂O (over 6-12 months)

Functional lifetime:
- Full effectiveness: 0-4 weeks
- Good effectiveness: 4-8 weeks
- Declining effectiveness: 8-12 weeks
- Minimal effectiveness: 12+ weeks

Implication: Product should be used within first 4-8 weeks for maximum benefit; after 12 weeks, mostly reduced to organic matter

PRODUCTION PROCESS:
═════════════════════

Step 1: Gather inputs
- Fresh pig slurry: 1,000 kg (collected fresh, refrigerated if delay >4 hours)

- Citric acid powder: 20 kg (food-grade, 99%+ purity)

Step 2: Prepare mixing vessel
- Container: 1,200+ liter capacity (can be open or closed)
- Material: Food-grade plastic or stainless steel (not aluminum)
- Mixing capability: Mechanical mixer or hand-mixing sufficient

- Add slurry to vessel first (base)
