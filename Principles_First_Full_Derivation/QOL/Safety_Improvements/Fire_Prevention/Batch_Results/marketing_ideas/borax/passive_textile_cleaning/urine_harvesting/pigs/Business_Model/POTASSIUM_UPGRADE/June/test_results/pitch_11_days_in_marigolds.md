## PRODUCT PROFILE

### Core Properties

**1. Advanced Water Retention**
APEX incorporates a professional-grade superabsorbent polymer system capable of retaining and slowly releasing hundreds of times its weight in water. Unlike standard soils that drain freely and require frequent irrigation, APEX creates a sustained moisture reservoir in the root zone — releasing water on demand as the plant needs it.

- Dramatically reduces irrigation frequency
- Eliminates drought stress between watering cycles
- Maintains consistent root zone moisture through heat, wind, and dry conditions
- Particularly critical for container growing, hanging baskets, and arid applications

**2. Slow-Release Nutrient Delivery**
APEX delivers a balanced NPK nutrient profile through a slow-release matrix embedded throughout the soil structure. Nutrients are not water-soluble and immediately leachable — they are locked into the amendment structure and released progressively as the root system develops.

- Sustained nitrogen availability supporting long vegetative and fruiting cycles
- Potassium enrichment driving flower quality, fruit size, and stress resistance
- Phosphorus support for root development and energy transfer
- No fertilizer burn risk at recommended application rates

**3. Enhanced Mineral Bioavailability**
A proprietary chelation system within the APEX matrix binds and protects micronutrients — iron, zinc, manganese, magnesium — from becoming locked up or leached away. These micronutrients are delivered directly to the root zone in plant-available form.

- Prevents micronutrient deficiency in alkaline or depleted soils
- Supports chlorophyll production, enzyme function, and stress defense
- Visible result: deeper green foliage, richer flower pigmentation

**4. Elevated Cation Exchange Capacity (CEC)**
APEX contains a high-CEC mineral component that increases the soil's ability to hold and exchange positively charged nutrient ions. This dramatically reduces nutrient leaching and increases fertilizer use efficiency.

- Nutrients stay in the root zone longer
- Reduces frequency and volume of fertilizer inputs
- Improves overall soil chemistry in degraded or poor quality base soils

**5. Structural Soil Support**
The mineral components in APEX improve soil aggregation, aeration, and drainage balance — preventing compaction while maintaining moisture retention. This creates an optimal physical environment for root development.
