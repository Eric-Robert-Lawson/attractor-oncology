═══════════════

Albedo = How much sun is reflected vs. absorbed
- Bare soil (light): High albedo = reflects heat (cooler)
- Bare soil (dark): Low albedo = absorbs heat (hotter)
- Vegetation: Low-to-medium albedo = absorbs some heat, transpires water
- Overall: More vegetation = cooler environment (transpiration cools)

YOUR HYDROGEL EFFECT:
────────────────────

Degraded land:
- Bare soil: High albedo (reflects heat)
- No vegetation: No transpiration cooling

Why? Transpiration cooling effect > albedo effect
