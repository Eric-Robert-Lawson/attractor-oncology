## PART 2: STORAGE & NATURAL PROCESSING

### What Happens When You Just Store Slurry

```
NATURAL PROCESSES IN STORED SLURRY:
═══════════════════════════════════

When you take slurry and store it in a massive area:

WATER LOSS (Evaporation):
- Exposed to sun/air: 10-20% water loss per month
- Stored in tanks: 2-5% water loss per month
- Over a year (outdoor storage): 50-80% water loss
- Effect: Slurry becomes more concentrated

NUTRIENT CONCENTRATION:
- As water evaporates, nutrients stay behind
- Nutrients concentrate 2-5x
- More valuable per unit volume

BACTERIAL PROCESSING:
- Aerobic decomposition: Reduces volume, creates heat
- Anaerobic processing: Creates biogas (methane)
- Net effect: Volume reduces, concentration increases

EXAMPLE (1,000 tons raw slurry, outdoor storage):

Starting state:
- Wet weight: 1,000 tons
- Water content: 90% = 900 tons water
- Dry matter: 10% = 100 tons nutrients

After 6 months outdoor storage:
- Water loss: 60% evaporation = 540 tons water lost
- Remaining: 460 tons (includes lost water + processing losses)
- Dry matter: Still ~80 tons (nutrients don't evaporate)
- Concentration: 80 ÷ 460 = 17.4% dry matter (vs. 10% initially)

MARKET VALUE CHANGE:
- Fresh slurry: 1,000 tons × $10/ton (your cost) = $10,000 spent
- Aged slurry: 460 tons × $30/ton (concentrated, valuable) = $13,800 revenue
- Profit: $3,800 just from storage

Over 12 months (full natural drying):
- Starting: 1,000 tons wet, cost $10,000
- After drying: 300 tons concentrated slurry (70% water loss)
- Dry matter: ~90 tons
- Concentration: 30% dry matter
- Market value: 300 tons × $40-50/ton = $12,000-15,000 revenue
- Profit: $2,000-5,000 (from just storing it)

THIS IS ACTUAL PROFIT WITH ZERO PROCESSING.

You buy waste, let time and nature concentrate it, sell it.
Profit: 20-50% on acquisition cost.
```
