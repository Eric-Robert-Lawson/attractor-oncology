**Use these for ANY dab batch:**

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
UNIVERSAL DAB PROTOCOL - ALL FORMULAS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

YOU PROVIDE:
════════════

D = Dab amount (grams) - How much dabs you have
P = Potency (%) - THC percentage of dabs (estimate if unknown)

Examples:
├─ D = 10g, P = 65% (your batch)
├─ D = 5g, P = 70% (another batch)
├─ D = 2g, P = 75% (smaller batch)
└─ D = 20g, P = 60% (larger batch)


SYSTEM CALCULATES:
══════════════════

Step 1: Calculate actual THC content
───────────────────────────────────

THC_total = D × (P ÷ 100)

Example: 10g at 65%
├─ THC_total = 10 × (65 ÷ 100) = 10 × 0.65 = 6.5g

Example: 5g at 70%
├─ THC_total = 5 × (70 ÷ 100) = 5 × 0.70 = 3.5g

Example: 2g at 75%
├─ THC_total = 2 × (75 ÷ 100) = 2 × 0.75 = 1.5g


Step 2: Calculate usable THC (accounting for 90% recovery)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

THC_usable = THC_total × 0.90

Example: 10g at 65%
├─ THC_usable = 6.5 × 0.90 = 5.85g THC available

Example: 5g at 70%
├─ THC_usable = 3.5 × 0.90 = 3.15g THC available

Example: 2g at 75%
├─ THC_usable = 1.5 × 0.90 = 1.35g THC available


Step 3: Calculate total dry powder needed
───────────────────────────────────────────

Powder_total = THC_usable × 1.20

(This is based on ratio from reference: 1.35g THC needs 1.7g powder)
(Ratio: 1.35 ÷ 1.70 = 0.794, which we round to 1.20 for simplicity)
(More precise: Powder_total = THC_usable × 1.26)

Example: 10g at 65%
├─ Powder_total = 5.85 × 1.20 = 7.02g ≈ 7g powder

Example: 5g at 70%
├─ Powder_total = 3.15 × 1.20 = 3.78g ≈ 3.8g powder

Example: 2g at 75%
├─ Powder_total = 1.35 × 1.20 = 1.62g ≈ 1.6g powder


Step 4: Break down powder into components
───────────────────────────────────────────

SAP = Powder_total × 0.296
Bentonite = Powder_total × 0.519
Baking_Soda = Powder_total × 0.185

Example: 10g at 65% (Powder_total = 7g)
├─ SAP = 7 × 0.296 = 2.07g ≈ 2.1g
├─ Bentonite = 7 × 0.519 = 3.63g ≈ 3.6g
├─ Baking_Soda = 7 × 0.185 = 1.30g ≈ 1.3g
└─ Total check: 2.1 + 3.6 + 1.3 = 7.0g ✓

Example: 5g at 70% (Powder_total = 3.8g)
├─ SAP = 3.8 × 0.296 = 1.12g ≈ 1.1g
├─ Bentonite = 3.8 × 0.519 = 1.97g ≈ 2.0g
├─ Baking_Soda = 3.8 × 0.185 = 0.70g ≈ 0.7g
└─ Total check: 1.1 + 2.0 + 0.7 = 3.8g ✓

Example: 2g at 75% (Powder_total = 1.6g)
├─ SAP = 1.6 × 0.296 = 0.47g ≈ 0.5g
├─ Bentonite = 1.6 × 0.519 = 0.83g ≈ 0.8g
├─ Baking_Soda = 1.6 × 0.185 = 0.30g ≈ 0.3g
└─ Total check: 0.5 + 0.8 + 0.3 = 1.6g ✓


Step 5: Calculate ethanol needed
──────────────────────────────────

Ethanol_dissolution = D × 12
Ethanol_hydration = Powder_total × 15

Total_ethanol = Ethanol_dissolution + Ethanol_hydration

Example: 10g at 65%
├─ Ethanol_dissolution = 10 × 12 = 120ml
├─ Ethanol_hydration = 7 × 15 = 105ml
├─ Total_ethanol = 120 + 105 = 225ml
└─ Practical: One 750ml bottle covers this + future

Example: 5g at 70%
├─ Ethanol_dissolution = 5 × 12 = 60ml
├─ Ethanol_hydration = 3.8 × 15 = 57ml
├─ Total_ethanol = 60 + 57 = 117ml
└─ Practical: One 750ml bottle covers this + future

Example: 2g at 75%
├─ Ethanol_dissolution = 2 × 12 = 24ml
├─ Ethanol_hydration = 1.6 × 15 = 24ml
├─ Total_ethanol = 24 + 24 = 48ml
└─ Practical: One 750ml bottle covers this + future


Step 6: Calculate water for application
─────────────────────────────────────────

Water = Powder_total × 5

(Add this water ONLY when ready to use/make aerogel, not now)

Example: 10g at 65%
├─ Water = 7 × 5 = 35ml

Example: 5g at 70%
├─ Water = 3.8 × 5 = 19ml

Example: 2g at 75%
├─ Water = 1.6 × 5 = 8ml


Step 7: Calculate final product specifications
────────────────────────────────────────────────

Dry_powder_stored = Powder_total (stored without water)

Final_aerogel_volume = Powder_total + Water

Final_potency = (THC_usable / Final_aerogel_volume) × 1000  [in mg/ml]

Example: 10g at 65%
├─ Dry_powder_stored = 7g
├─ Final_aerogel_volume = 7g + 35ml = 42ml
├─ Final_potency = (5.85 / 42) × 1000 = 139 mg/ml
└─ Per application (0.75g powder): 5.85g × (0.75/7) = 0.63g ≈ 630mg THC

Example: 5g at 70%

├─ Final_aerogel_volume = 3.8g + 19ml = 22.8ml
├─ Final_potency = (3.15 / 22.8) × 1000 = 138 mg/ml
└─ Per application (0.75g powder): 3.15g × (0.75/3.8) = 0.62g ≈ 620mg THC

Example: 2g at 75%
├─ Dry_powder_stored = 1.6g
├─ Final_aerogel_volume = 1.6g + 8ml = 9.6ml
├─ Final_potency = (1.35 / 9.6) × 1000 = 141 mg/ml
└─ Per application (0.75g powder): 1.35g × (0.75/1.6) = 0.63g ≈ 630mg THC


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SUMMARY - ALL FORMULAS IN ONE PLACE
```

### 4.1 — How To Estimate Your Dab Potency

**If you don't know the exact potency:**

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ESTIMATING DAB POTENCY (If you don't know)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

DECISION TREE:
══════════════

Are your dabs high-quality/recent?
├─ YES → Estimate 75-85% THC
├─ MAYBE → Estimate 70-75% THC
└─ NO (old/degraded) → Estimate 60-70% THC


Visual/quality clues:

Clear, translucent, golden (shatter/diamonds):
├─ Likely potency: 80-90%
├─ Use for calculation: 80% (conservative)
└─ Example: "High-grade shatter"

Cloudy, waxy, budder consistency:
├─ Likely potency: 70-80%
├─ Use for calculation: 75% (middle estimate)
└─ Example: "Medium-grade budder"

Dark, brownish, old appearance:
├─ Likely potency: 60-70%
├─ Use for calculation: 65% (conservative)
└─ Example: "Older or lower-grade dabs"

Very dark, degraded, or oxidized:
├─ Likely potency: 50-65%
├─ Use for calculation: 60% (conservative)
└─ Example: "Very old dabs (1+ year)"


For your 10g batch (1+ year old):
├─ Appearance: Likely darker/degraded
├─ Potency estimate: 60-70%
├─ Recommendation: Use 65% (conservative middle)
└─ Reasoning: Accounts for oxidation over time


IF YOU'RE UNSURE:
═════════════════

Use 65% as default:
├─ Conservative (not too optimistic)
├─ Accounts for some degradation
├─ Safe middle ground
└─ If you guess too high: Product is fine (just more of it)
└─ If you guess too low: Product is fine (just less of it)


POTENCY RANGES BY TYPE:
════════════════════════

Diamonds/clear shatter: 85-95%
High-grade shatter: 80-90%
Regular shatter/crumble: 75-85%
Budder/wax (quality): 70-80%
Budder/wax (regular): 65-75%
Live resin: 70-85%
Rosin (solventless): 60-80%
Old/degraded (1+ year): 50-70%
Very old/oxidized (2+ year): 40-65%


RECOMMENDATION:
════════════════

When in doubt: Estimate CONSERVATIVE (lower end)
├─ Reason 1: Better to have extra product than less
├─ Reason 2: More potent final product is only benefit
├─ Reason 3: Can always make stronger by using less water
└─ Result: Start low, you'll be pleasantly surprised
```

## PART VII: TROUBLESHOOTING & ADJUSTMENTS

### 7.1 — Common Variations

**What if your situation is different:**

```
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ADJUSTMENTS & VARIATIONS
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: What if I want a LESS potent final product?
═════════════════════════════════════════════════

Option 1: Increase final water amount
├─ Standard: Water = Powder_total × 5
├─ Less potent: Water = Powder_total × 7 or 8 or 10
├─ More dilute: More water = lower concentration
└─ Example: Add 50ml instead of 35ml = more dilute

Option 2: Use less powder per application
├─ Standard: 0.75g powder per application
├─ Less potent: 0.5g powder per application
└─ Result: Same product, just use less

Option 3: Recalculate with lower potency estimate
├─ Use lower P value in formulas
├─ Example: Assume 60% instead of 65%
├─ Result: Makes less total powder
└─ Effect: Less concentrated final product


Q: What if I want a MORE potent final product?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Option 1: Decrease final water amount
├─ Standard: Water = Powder_total × 5
├─ More potent: Water = Powder_total × 3 or 4
├─ Less dilute: Less water = higher concentration
└─ Example: Add 20ml instead of 35ml = more potent

Option 2: Use more powder per application
├─ Standard: 0.75g powder per application
├─ More potent: 1.0g powder per application
└─ Result: Same product, use more

Option 3: Recalculate with higher potency estimate
├─ Use higher P value in formulas
├─ Example: Assume 75% instead of 65%
├─ Result: Makes more total powder
└─ Effect: More concentrated final product


Q: What if my dabs are REALLY OLD (2+ years)?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Use conservative potency: 50-60% (or even lower)
├─ Reasoning: THC degrades over time
├─ Safety: Better to estimate low
├─ Result: Won't disappoint (may have more)
└─ Formula: Same, just use lower P value


Q: What if my dabs are VERY HIGH QUALITY (shatter)?
════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Use higher potency: 85-90%
├─ Reasoning: Shatter is typically highest potency
├─ Result: More concentrated product
└─ Formula: Same, just use higher P value


Q: What if I want to mix with HEMP FLOWER or other material?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This formula is specifically for DABS ONLY
├─ Dabs are already extracted
├─ Hemp flower needs extraction first
├─ Don't mix: Use separate protocols
└─ Recommendation: Use dab protocol for dabs, flower protocol for flower


Q: Can I use the FLOWER PROTOCOL INSTEAD of this dab protocol?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Yes, but you'll need to convert dabs to equivalent flower:
├─ Convert: D ÷ (P ÷ 30) to get flower equivalent
├─ Use flower protocol with that value
├─ But: More complicated than direct dab protocol
└─ Recommendation: Use dab protocol instead (this one)


Q: What if I don't have a scale and can't measure accurately?
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Estimates work fine:
├─ ±0.1-0.2g error is acceptable
├─ Use measuring spoons as rough guide
├─ SAP 0.296g ≈ slightly less than 1/2 teaspoon
├─ Bentonite 0.519g ≈ about 1 teaspoon
├─ Baking soda small amount ≈ 1/4 teaspoon

├─ Lasts forever
└─ Worth the investment
```
