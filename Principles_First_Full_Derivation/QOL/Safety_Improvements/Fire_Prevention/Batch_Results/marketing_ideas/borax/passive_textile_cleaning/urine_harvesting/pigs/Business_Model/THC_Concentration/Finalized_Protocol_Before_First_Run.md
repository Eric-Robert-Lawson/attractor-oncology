Formula: V_final = (X ÷ 3) + W

├─ X ÷ 3 = dry powder weight (in grams, ~equals ml when hydrated)
├─ W = water you add (in ml)

FINAL POTENCY CALCULATION
═════════════════════════════

Estimated THC concentration (mg/ml):
Formula: Potency = [(X × Y × 0.90) ÷ V_final] × 1000

Where:
├─ X = cannabis amount (grams)
├─ Y = THC potency (as decimal, e.g., 0.30 for 30%)
├─ 0.90 = extraction efficiency (90%)
├─ V_final = final volume (ml)
├─ 1000 = conversion to mg

Simplified:
└─ Potency (mg/ml) = (X × Y × 900) ÷ V_final

Examples:

X = 5g at 30% THC, W = 25ml:
├─ Potency = (5 × 0.30 × 900) ÷ 25 = 1350 ÷ 25 = 54 mg/ml
├─ Actual: ~36-54 mg/ml (accounting for some variation)
└─ Per 10ml dose: 360-540mg THC

X = 10g at 25% THC, W = 50ml:
├─ Potency = (10 × 0.25 × 900) ÷ 50 = 2250 ÷ 50 = 45 mg/ml
├─ Actual: ~30-45 mg/ml
└─ Per 10ml dose: 300-450mg THC

X = 20g at 30% THC, W = 75ml:
├─ Potency = (20 × 0.30 × 900) ÷ 75 = 5400 ÷ 75 = 72 mg/ml
├─ Actual: ~45-72 mg/ml
└─ Per 10ml dose: 450-720mg THC
