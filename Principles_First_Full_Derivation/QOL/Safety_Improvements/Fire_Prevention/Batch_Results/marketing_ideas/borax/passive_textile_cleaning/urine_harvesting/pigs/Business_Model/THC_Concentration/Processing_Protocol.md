## STAGE 1: ETHANOL EXTRACTION CALCULATIONS

### 1.1 — Determining Ethanol Quantity Needed

**For complete extraction:**

```
ETHANOL EXTRACTION RATIO:
═════════════════════════

Standard ethanol extraction requires:
├─ Optimal ratio: 1 part cannabis : 10-15 parts ethanol (by volume)
├─ Conservative: 1:15 (more ethanol, ensure complete saturation)
├─ Aggressive: 1:10 (less ethanol, still effective)
└─ Recommended: 1:12 (balance between complete extraction and efficiency)

CALCULATION FOR ANY AMOUNT OF CANNABIS:
═════════════════════════════════════════

Formula: Ethanol needed (ml) = Cannabis weight (g) × 12

Examples:
├─ 5g cannabis → 5 × 12 = 60ml ethanol
├─ 10g cannabis → 10 × 12 = 120ml ethanol
├─ 15g cannabis → 15 × 12 = 180ml ethanol
├─ 20g cannabis → 20 × 12 = 240ml ethanol
├─ 25g cannabis → 25 × 12 = 300ml ethanol
└─ 30g cannabis → 30 × 12 = 360ml ethanol

PRACTICAL GUIDANCE:
════════════════════

Buy slightly more ethanol than calculated:
├─ Calculate needed amount
├─ Add 10-20% buffer (for loss, spillage)
├─ Example: 10g cannabis needs 120ml, buy 150ml total
└─ Ensures you have enough

For first extraction:
├─ Use conservative ratio (1:15)
├─ Better to have extra ethanol
├─ Ensures complete extraction
├─ Extra evaporates off anyway
└─ Recommended: Buy 1.5x calculated amount
```

## STAGE 2: POWDER FORMULATION & HYDRATION

### 2.1 — Dry Powder Mix Formulation

**Before ethanol hydration:**

```
DRY POWDER FORMULA (Pre-hydration):
═══════════════════════════════════

Ratio: SAP : Bentonite Clay : Baking Soda = 8:14:5
(This ratio is optimized for aerogel properties)

Total parts: 8 + 14 + 5 = 27 parts

For any batch size:
├─ SAP = (8/27) × Total powder weight
├─ Bentonite = (14/27) × Total powder weight
├─ Baking Soda = (5/27) × Total powder weight
└─ Example: For 27g total powder:
   ├─ SAP: 8g
   ├─ Bentonite: 14g
   └─ Baking Soda: 5g

CALCULATION FOR ANY CANNABIS AMOUNT:
══════════════════════════════════════

Optimal dry powder amount:
Formula: Dry powder needed (g) = Cannabis weight (g) ÷ 3

Examples:
├─ 5g cannabis → 5÷3 = 1.67g powder (round to 1.7g or 2g)
├─ 10g cannabis → 10÷3 = 3.33g powder (round to 3.3g or 3.5g)
├─ 15g cannabis → 15÷3 = 5g powder
├─ 20g cannabis → 20÷3 = 6.67g powder (round to 6.7g or 7g)
├─ 25g cannabis → 25÷3 = 8.33g powder
├─ 30g cannabis → 30÷3 = 10g powder
└─ Rule: Always round UP (better to have slightly more powder)

SPECIFIC POWDER MIXTURE CALCULATIONS:
══════════════════════════════════════

Example 1: 10g cannabis → 3.5g total powder
├─ SAP: 3.5 × (8/27) = 1.04g ≈ 1g
├─ Bentonite: 3.5 × (14/27) = 1.81g ≈ 1.8g
├─ Baking Soda: 3.5 × (5/27) = 0.65g ≈ 0.7g
└─ Total: ~3.5g (verify by adding back: 1 + 1.8 + 0.7 = 3.5g ✓)

Example 2: 20g cannabis → 7g total powder
├─ SAP: 7 × (8/27) = 2.07g ≈ 2g
├─ Bentonite: 7 × (14/27) = 3.63g ≈ 3.6g
├─ Baking Soda: 7 × (5/27) = 1.30g ≈ 1.3g
└─ Total: ~7g (verify: 2 + 3.6 + 1.3 = 6.9g ≈ 7g ✓)

GENERAL FORMULA FOR ANY AMOUNT:
═════════════════════════════════

For X grams of cannabis:

Dry powder weight: P = X ÷ 3 grams

Then divide P into:
├─ SAP: P × 0.296 grams (or P × 8/27)
├─ Bentonite: P × 0.519 grams (or P × 14/27)
├─ Baking Soda: P × 0.185 grams (or P × 5/27)
└─ Total: P grams (should equal original calculation)

PREPARATION OF DRY POWDER MIX:
═══════════════════════════════

Step 1: Measure SAP, bentonite, and baking soda
├─ Use scale (accurate to 0.1g)
├─ Measure each component separately
└─ Verify total weight equals calculated amount

Step 2: Mix thoroughly
├─ Combine all three in bowl
├─ Stir/blend until uniform
├─ No visible clumping
├─ Result: Dry powder mix ready for hydration
└─ Status: Can be stored dry until needed
```

### 2.2 — Powder Hydration With Enriched Ethanol

**Combining dry powder with extracted ethanol:**

```
HYDRATION CALCULATION:
═════════════════════

Ratio: Dry powder to ethanol = 1:15 (weight:volume)

Formula: Ethanol volume for hydration (ml) = Dry powder weight (g) × 15

Examples:
├─ 1g powder → 1 × 15 = 15ml ethanol
├─ 3.5g powder (for 10g cannabis) → 3.5 × 15 = 52.5ml ethanol
├─ 5g powder → 5 × 15 = 75ml ethanol
├─ 7g powder (for 20g cannabis) → 7 × 15 = 105ml ethanol
├─ 10g powder → 10 × 15 = 150ml ethanol
└─ Scales proportionally


IMPORTANT NOTE - ETHANOL TRACKING:
═══════════════════════════════════

You have enriched ethanol from Stage 1:
├─ Total enriched ethanol: ~X × 12 ml (from Stage 1 calculation)
├─ Used for powder hydration: ~P × 15 ml (calculated above)
├─ Remaining in container: Difference

This is normal - some enriched ethanol stays in container below powder,
some gets absorbed into powder.

Both contain THC (some in powder, some in remaining ethanol).

This is intentional (two-phase system).


HYDRATION PROCESS:
═══════════════════

Step 1: Get hydrated powder mixture
├─ Take dry powder mix (prepared in Stage 2.1)
├─ Pour calculated amount of enriched ethanol
├─ Mix thoroughly (spoon or blender can work)
├─ Result: Ethanol-saturated powder
└─ Consistency: Wet, paste-like

Step 2: Status after hydration
├─ Powder: Fully hydrated with ethanol + THC
├─ SAP: Swollen (expanded ~3x original size)
├─ Contains: All THC from enriched ethanol
└─ Ready for: Next stage (evaporation)
```

### 2.3 — Ethanol Evaporation

**Removing ethanol, leaving THC in powder:**

```
EVAPORATION PARAMETERS:
═══════════════════════

Goal: Remove ethanol while preserving THC in powder
Method: Passive evaporation (safest for THC compounds)

Setup:
├─ Container: Shallow dish (spreads ethanol thin)
├─ Location: Well-ventilated area (window open)
├─ Temperature: Room temperature (65-75°F)
├─ Safety: No heat applied (preserves terpenes)
└─ Duration: 6-24 hours (depending on amount, temperature, humidity)

EVAPORATION TIMELINE:
══════════════════════

Amount to evaporate (using 10g cannabis example):
├─ Ethanol to evaporate: ~52ml
├─ Evaporation rate: ~5-8ml per hour at room temp
├─ Estimated time: 6-10 hours

Timeline:
├─ Hour 0-2: Ethanol clearly visible, strong smell
├─ Hour 2-4: Level dropping noticeably
├─ Hour 4-8: Mostly evaporated, faint smell
├─ Hour 8-12: Appears dry, no liquid visible
└─ Hour 12+: Fully dry (all ethanol gone)

VERIFICATION:
═══════════════

How to know evaporation is complete:
├─ Visual: No liquid visible in mixture
├─ Smell: No ethanol aroma (or very faint)
├─ Touch: Mixture not wet (maybe slightly damp is OK)
├─ Time: Minimum 6 hours, verify at each step
└─ Result: Powder + THC mixture, ready for rehydration

RESULT AFTER EVAPORATION:
═══════════════════════════

What you have:
├─ Powder particles: SAP, bentonite, baking soda
├─ THC/compounds: Absorbed into powder interior
├─ Weight: ~(original powder + THC weight)
├─ Appearance: Dark brown/black powder or paste
├─ Status: Ready for water rehydration
└─ Quantity: ~X/3 grams (from original cannabis ratio)

Example with 10g cannabis:
├─ Original dry powder: ~3.5g
├─ Plus THC absorbed: ~0.2-0.3g
├─ Final dry product: ~3.7-3.8g
└─ Status: Ready for final rehydration
```

## STAGE 3: AEROGEL CREATION & REHYDRATION

### 3.1 — Water Rehydration Calculation

**Creating final aerogel with water:**

```
WATER REHYDRATION RATIO:
═════════════════════════

Ratio: Water volume to dry THC-powder = 3.5:1 (volume:weight)

Formula: Water needed (ml) = Dry THC-powder weight (g) × 3.5

This creates aerogel consistency (gel-like, not liquid, not solid).


CALCULATION FOR ANY CANNABIS AMOUNT:
══════════════════════════════════════

Step 1: Determine final dry product weight
├─ Dry powder: X ÷ 3 grams (from Stage 2)
├─ Plus absorbed THC: ~(X ÷ 3) × 0.05 to 0.08g
├─ Total: (X ÷ 3) × 1.06 grams (approximately)
└─ Simplified: Just use X ÷ 3 (close enough, THC adds little weight)

Step 2: Calculate water needed
├─ Water = (X ÷ 3) × 3.5 ml
└─ Or: Water = X × (3.5 ÷ 3) = X × 1.167 ml

Examples:
├─ 5g cannabis → powder ~1.67g → water needed: 1.67 × 3.5 = 5.85ml ≈ 6ml
├─ 10g cannabis → powder ~3.3g → water needed: 3.3 × 3.5 = 11.55ml ≈ 12ml
├─ 15g cannabis → powder ~5g → water needed: 5 × 3.5 = 17.5ml
├─ 20g cannabis → powder ~6.67g → water needed: 6.67 × 3.5 = 23.35ml ≈ 23ml
├─ 25g cannabis → powder ~8.33g → water needed: 8.33 × 3.5 = 29.15ml ≈ 29ml
├─ 30g cannabis → powder ~10g → water needed: 10 × 3.5 = 35ml
└─ 50g cannabis → powder ~16.67g → water needed: 16.67 × 3.5 = 58.35ml ≈ 58ml


QUICK FORMULA:
═══════════════

For X grams of cannabis:
├─ Water needed (ml) = X × 1.167 ml
├─ Or approximately: X × 1.2 ml (conservative, adds slight extra)
└─ Examples:
   ├─ 10g cannabis → ~12ml water
   ├─ 20g cannabis → ~23ml water
   ├─ 30g cannabis → ~35ml water
   └─ 50g cannabis → ~58ml water
```

### 3.2 — Aerogel Creation Process

**Making final product:**

```
AEROGEL FORMULATION:
════════════════════

Final recipe (for any amount):

Ingredients (per X grams cannabis):
├─ Dry THC-powder: (X ÷ 3) grams
├─ Water: (X × 1.167) ml
├─ Optional: Glycerin: 10% by final volume (improves texture)
├─ Optional: Menthol oil: 2-5 drops (penetration enhancer)
└─ Total product: ~(X ÷ 3 + X × 1.167) ≈ X × 1.5 ml (approximate)

GLYCERIN ADDITION (Optional but recommended):
═════════════════════════════════════════════

Glycerin improves:
├─ Texture (smoother, more spreadable)
├─ Hydration (better skin absorption)
├─ Stability (longer shelf life)
└─ Consistency (prevents drying out)

Ratio: Add 10% glycerin by final volume

Example with 10g cannabis:
├─ Dry powder: 3.3g
├─ Water: 12ml
├─ If using glycerin:
│  ├─ Expected final volume: ~15ml
│  ├─ Glycerin to add: 15 × 0.10 = 1.5ml
│  └─ Adjusted water: 12 - 1.5 = 10.5ml (water + glycerin = 11.5ml)
└─ Final product: ~3.3g powder + ~11.5ml liquid = ~14.8ml total


MENTHOL OIL ADDITION (Optional for pain relief):
═════════════════════════════════════════════════

Menthol enhances:
├─ Topical penetration (proven enhancer)
├─ Cooling sensation (pleasant sensory)
├─ Pain relief (synergistic with THC)
└─ Aroma (mint scent)

Ratio: 2-5 drops per 30ml product

Example: For 14.8ml product from 10g cannabis
├─ Drops to add: ~1-2 drops (scale proportionally)
└─ Optional (can skip if desired)


AEROGEL CREATION - STEP BY STEP:
═════════════════════════════════

Step 1: Prepare liquid base
├─ Measure water (calculated amount)
├─ If using glycerin: Replace 10% of water with glycerin
│  ├─ Measure glycerin
│  ├─ Measure remaining water
│  └─ Combine
├─ Optional: Add menthol drops
└─ Result: Liquid base ready

Step 2: Add dry THC-powder to liquid
├─ Pour liquid into bowl or container
├─ Add dry THC-powder mixture
├─ Mix gently with spoon
└─ Result: Hydrating powder starting to form

Step 3: Blend to aerogel consistency
├─ Use immersion blender (recommended)
│  ├─ Blend for 30-60 seconds
│  ├─ Until uniform aerogel forms
│  ├─ Should be smooth, gel-like
│  └─ No visible powder chunks
├─ Or: Mix vigorously with spoon/whisk
│  ├─ Mix for 1-2 minutes
│  ├─ Until as uniform as possible
│  └─ OK if not perfectly smooth
└─ Result: Final aerogel product

Step 4: Verify consistency
├─ Spreadable: Should spread easily (not liquid)
├─ Holds shape: Should hold form when applied
├─ Hydration: SAP should feel hydrated (not gritty)
├─ Aroma: Should smell like cannabis + water/herbal
└─ Status: Ready to package or use

TROUBLESHOOTING CONSISTENCY:
═════════════════════════════

Too thick/dry:
├─ Add more water (5ml at a time)
├─ Blend again
└─ Repeat until desired consistency

Too liquid/runny:
├─ Add more dry powder (0.5-1g at a time)
├─ Or: Add more SAP powder to absorb liquid
├─ Blend again
└─ Repeat until firmer

Too gritty/sandy:
├─ Blend longer (immersion blender 1-2 minutes)
├─ Or: Mix very thoroughly with spoon
├─ Result: Usually becomes smoother
└─ Normal: Some texture OK for topical use
```
