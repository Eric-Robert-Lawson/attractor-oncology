## WHAT HAPPENS AT EACH STAGE

### Understanding Your Process:

```
STAGE 1: ETHANOL EXTRACTION - WHAT'S HAPPENING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Your action: Pour ethanol on cannabis, wait 30-40 min

What's happening:
├─ Ethanol molecules penetrate cannabis material
├─ THC dissolves into ethanol (lipophilic compounds in lipophilic solvent)
├─ Other compounds also dissolve (terpenes, chlorophyll, plant compounds)
├─ Result: Ethanol is now enriched with dissolved compounds
├─ Appearance: Liquid turns green/brown (darker over time)
└─ Chemistry: THC now exists as dissolved molecules in ethanol


STAGE 2: FILTERING - WHAT'S HAPPENING
══════════════════════════════════════

Your action: Pour through pillowcase into new container, squeeze

What's happening:
├─ Pillowcase membrane blocks large particles (cannabis material)
├─ Small ethanol molecules pass through (easily)
├─ Small THC molecules pass through WITH ethanol (they're dissolved)
├─ Result: Ethanol + dissolved THC in new container (clean)
├─ Cannabis material: Stays in pillowcase/baggie (discarded)
├─ Purity: 100% clean ethanol extraction
└─ Result: Very clean enriched ethanol ready for powder


STAGE 3: POWDER MIXING - WHAT'S HAPPENING
══════════════════════════════════════════

Your action: Mix dry powder into enriched ethanol

What's happening:
├─ Powder particles come into contact with enriched ethanol
├─ SAP (hydrophilic polymer) absorbs ethanol
├─ Bentonite (clay) absorbs ethanol
├─ Ethanol gets absorbed throughout powder structure
├─ THC dissolved in ethanol goes WITH ethanol into powder
├─ Result: Wet, gel-like mixture (ethanol + powder + THC all combined)
├─ Appearance: Thick paste, dark color
└─ Chemistry: Powder now contains ethanol + THC absorbed throughout


STAGE 4: EVAPORATION - WHAT'S HAPPENING
════════════════════════════════════════

Your action: Spread in dish, wait for ethanol to evaporate

What's happening:
├─ Ethanol molecules at surface evaporate (78°C boiling point)
├─ Air circulation removes ethanol vapor
├─ Remaining ethanol evaporates progressively
├─ THC stays behind (157°C boiling point, doesn't evaporate)
├─ Terpenes mostly stay (some may volatilize)
├─ Plant compounds stay behind
├─ Result: Powder becomes dry, ethanol is gone
├─ THC location: Still in/on powder particles (absorbed during Stage 3)
└─ Chemistry: Dry powder now contains all the THC + compounds from your cannabis


STAGE 5: REHYDRATION - WHAT'S HAPPENING
════════════════════════════════════════

Your action: Add water, blend together

What's happening:
├─ Water added to dry THC-powder
├─ Blending breaks up powder particles
├─ Water and powder mix into uniform consistency
├─ SAP rehydrates (swells as it absorbs water)
├─ Result: Smooth aerogel (water + powder + THC all blended)
├─ Appearance: Smooth, gel-like, dark colored
└─ Chemistry: Final product is hydrated polymer gel containing THC/compounds


FINAL RESULT: THERMAL AEROGEL
══════════════════════════════

What you have:
├─ ~7.5ml gel-like concentrate
├─ Contains: SAP + bentonite + baking soda + water + THC + terpenes + plant compounds
├─ Color: Dark brown/black
├─ Aroma: Strong cannabis
├─ Consistency: Spreadable, gel-like
├─ Potency: ~180 mg/ml THC (VERY POTENT)
├─ Ready to: Use topically with heat OR dilute for easier dosing
└─ Duration when used: 3-4 hours pain relief expected
```
