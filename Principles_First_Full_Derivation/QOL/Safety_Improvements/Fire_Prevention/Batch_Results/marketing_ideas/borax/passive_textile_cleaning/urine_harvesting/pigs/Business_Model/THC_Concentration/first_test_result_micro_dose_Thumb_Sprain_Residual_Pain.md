## DOCUMENTED TRIAL: SPRAINED THUMB APPLICATION

### Trial Parameters

**Subject:** Sprained thumb/wrist
- **Injury type:** Joint/muscle sprain (interphalangeal joint complex)
- **Injury mechanism:** Flexion injury causing muscular strain on ventral thumb surface
- **Pain location:** Underside of thumb (flexor compartment) - pain when thumb flexed backward toward palm
- **Pain onset:** Pre-trial baseline pain present
- **Pain character:** Muscular/joint pain from sprain injury
- **Injury age:** Residual (sufficient healing for topical application)

**Material Applied:**
- **Source:** Residual ethanol-wet dab paste from batch processing (waste material)
- **Composition:** THC/CBD + ethanol + SAP residue (not pure dry powder - important variable)
- **Quantity:** Approximately half pinky-fingernail-sized blob (~0.1-0.2g estimated)
- **Water added:** Few drops (minimal, not soaked - estimated 2-5ml)
- **Containment:** Wax paper
- **Application site:** Directly on sprain area (underside of thumb)

**Heat Application:**
- **Method:** Direct warm water flow from sink faucet
- **Temperature:** Approximately 38-42°C (warm, not hot, comfortable to hold)
- **Duration:** 15-20 minutes continuous
- **Technique:** Water ran directly onto wax paper over application site
- **Saturation:** Wax paper became fully saturated with warm water

**Timeline:**
- **0 minutes:** Application complete, heat begins
- **5-10 minutes:** Initial sensation, relief beginning to register
- **15-20 minutes:** Heat applied continuously, relief developing
- **20 minutes:** Removed wax paper, observed paste formation
- **Post-removal:** Paste remains on skin, pain observation initiated
