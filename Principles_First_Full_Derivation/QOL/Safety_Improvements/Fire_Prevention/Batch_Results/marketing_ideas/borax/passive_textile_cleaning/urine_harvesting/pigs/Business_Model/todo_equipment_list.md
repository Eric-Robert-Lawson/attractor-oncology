NEGOTIATION STRATEGY FOR USED EQUIPMENT:
═════════════════════════════════════════

[ ] 29. Learn negotiation tactics for used equipment
        NOTE: Used equipment is negotiable (new equipment is not)
        
        GENERAL NEGOTIATION PRINCIPLES:
        
        Principle 1: Identify room for negotiation
        [ ] Ask seller: "Is this your firm price or negotiable?"
        [ ] If new dealer: Firm price (no negotiation)
        [ ] If used private seller: Often 10-30% negotiable
        [ ] If equipment broker: Often 10-20% negotiable
        
        Principle 2: Research market price
        [ ] Before negotiating, know market price
        [ ] Check 3-5 listings of same model
        [ ] Understand if this is good/average/poor price
        [ ] Example: "XSG-2 dryers typically $3,500-5,000, 
                     yours at $4,200 seems high"
        
        Principle 3: Point out condition issues (professionally)
        [ ] "I notice the paint is chipped and controls look worn"
        [ ] "What's the service history on this unit?"
        [ ] "Has this been well-maintained?"
        [ ] Don't be rude; be factual
        
        Principle 4: Make an offer
        [ ] Start 15-25% below asking price
        [ ] "I'm interested, but I need to stay under budget.
             Would you take $3,000 instead of $3,500?"
        [ ] If they counter: meet in middle
        [ ] Example: They want $3,500, you offer $3,000
                     They counter $3,300, you accept $3,250
        
        Principle 5: Bundle negotiations
        [ ] If buying multiple items from same seller: "Bundle deal?"
        [ ] Sellers often give discount for multiple items
        [ ] Example: "If I buy the blender AND the pump for $2,200 total, 
                     would that work?" (vs. $2,500 separate)
        
        Principle 6: Payment method flexibility
        [ ] Cash deals: Often get 5-10% discount ("Cash only price?")
        [ ] Pickup only: No shipping cost to you, can negotiate
        [ ] As-is: Buyer beware = lower price
        
        DO'S AND DON'Ts:
        
        ✓ DO: Be respectful and professional
        ✓ DO: Ask about maintenance history
        ✓ DO: Request videos/pictures of operation
        ✓ DO: Understand why equipment is being sold
        ✓ DO: Ask about warranty (even used often has 30-90 day)
        ✓ DO: Get seller's contact info (future issues)
        ✓ DO: Inspect equipment before final payment (if local)
        
        ✗ DON'T: Be disrespectful or insulting
        ✗ DON'T: Lowball absurdly (insults seller)
        ✗ DON'T: Agree to price then back out
        ✗ DON'T: Pay before inspecting (if possible)
        ✗ DON'T: Send money to unknown seller (use PayPal/escrow)
        ✗ DON'T: Assume equipment is "as advertised" (verify)
        
        Status: Negotiation strategy understood? YES / NO
