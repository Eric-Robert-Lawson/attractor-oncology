
- Insurance (product liability).
- Conservative entry (start with lowest
  risk applications, expand as data
  accumulates).

RISK 2: REGULATORY CHANGES

If borate is classified as hazardous
in certain jurisdictions:

Cost: Market access loss in those
regions, reformulation costs.

Mitigation:
- Monitor regulatory environment.
- Develop alternative borax-free
  formulations (slightly reduced
  efficacy, but acceptable).
- Diversify geography (if one region
  bans borate, others allow).

────────────────────────────────────────

RISK 3: COMMODITY PRICE COLLAPSE

If fertiliser prices fall 50%:

Cost: Competitive pressure in
agriculture segment.

Mitigation:
- This is only 15% of revenue at
  mature stage.
- Can absorb price reduction and
  maintain profitability on agriculture
  at lower margin.
- Shift volume toward higher-margin
  applications.

────────────────────────────────────────

RISK 4: COMPETITOR TECHNOLOGY

If competitor develops superior
product (better properties, lower cost):

Cost: Market share loss.

Mitigation:
- Continuous R&D (improve product,
  discover new applications).
- Patent portfolio (protect unique
  formulations).
- Customer lock-in (switching costs).
- Scale advantages (hard to match).

────────────────────────────────────────

RISK 5: SUPPLY CHAIN DISRUPTION

If SAP or bentonite supply is disrupted:

Cost: Production halt, supply shortfall.

Mitigation:
- Vertical integration (own SAP and
  bentonite production facilities).
- Supplier diversification (multiple
  sourcing regions).
- Inventory buffer (maintain 6-month
  supply).

────────────────────────────────────────

RISK 6: MARKET ACCEPTANCE

If customers in new applications
(medical, cosmetics) reject product:

Cost: Failed market entry, R&D costs
wasted.

Mitigation:
- Pilot programs (test product in
  customer hands before full launch).
- Customer co-development (partner
  with early adopters, refine product).
- Conservative launch (start with
  applications where product has
  strongest value proposition).

────────────────────────────────────────

RISK 7: ENVIRONMENTAL/SOCIAL
BACKLASH

If public perception turns against
P1S-B products due to urine sourcing:

Cost: Brand damage, market rejection.

Mitigation:
- Transparency (disclose sourcing,
  emphasize closed-loop benefits).
- Sterilisation (ensure product is
  pathogen-free through quality control).
- Branding (position as regenerative,
  circular economy leader, not "urine
  product").

────────────────────────────────────────

OVERALL RISK ASSESSMENT:

Risks are MANAGEABLE through standard
business practices.

No single risk eliminates the business
model.

Multiple revenue streams provide
resilience (if one application fails,
others compensate).

Competitive advantages (scale, patents,
relationships) are defensible.

Risk profile: MODERATE.

Reward/risk ratio: EXCEPTIONAL.
