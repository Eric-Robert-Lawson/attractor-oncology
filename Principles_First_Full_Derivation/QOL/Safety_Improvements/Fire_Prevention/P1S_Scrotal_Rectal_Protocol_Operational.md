## P1S-SCROTAL Preparation Steps

```
PREPARATION TIME: 15-20 minutes
EQUIPMENT NEEDED: Bowl, whisk, scale, pH strips, thermometer

STEP 1 — PREPARE AGENT SOLUTION:
  Measure 500mL distilled water into mixing bowl.
  Water temperature: 40-45°C (warm but not hot —
  helps dissolve agents, does not degrade them).
  
  Add agents IN THIS ORDER, stirring until dissolved
  between each addition:
  
    1. Niacinamide 1.0g — dissolves quickly, clear solution
    2. Sodium Ascorbate 1.0g — dissolves quickly
    3. Panthenol 1.0g — may need brief stirring
    4. N-Acetyl Cysteine 0.4g — dissolves with stirring,
       slight sulfur smell is normal
    5. Sodium R-ALA 0.2g — stir until fully dissolved
       (sodium salt form dissolves readily in warm water)
    6. Pyridoxine HCl 0.05g — dissolves immediately
    7. Biotin 0.02g — trace amount, stir thoroughly
    
  RESULT: Clear to slightly yellow solution.
  The riboflavin-adjacent agents give slight yellow tint.
  This is normal.

STEP 2 — pH CHECK AND ADJUSTMENT:
  Test solution pH with pH strip.
  Expected pH before adjustment: approximately 6.0-7.5
  (sodium ascorbate is mildly alkaline, NAC is mildly acidic)
  
  TARGET pH FOR SCROTAL: 5.5-6.5
  (matches scrotal skin pH environment)
  
  IF pH IS ABOVE 6.5:
    Add citric acid in 0.1g increments.
    Stir. Test. Repeat until target reached.
    
  IF pH IS BELOW 5.5:
    Add small pinch of sodium bicarbonate (~0.05g).
    Stir. Test. Adjust.
    
  RECORD FINAL pH for batch quality tracking.

STEP 3 — ADD FUMED SILICA:
  PUT ON N95 RESPIRATOR BEFORE OPENING SILICA CONTAINER.
  
  While stirring the solution continuously,
  slowly sift 20g fumed silica into the warm solution.
  Add slowly — do not dump all at once.
  Whisk vigorously for 2 minutes until fully dispersed.
  No visible dry silica powder should remain.
  
  The solution will become slightly cloudy/milky.
  This is correct.
  
  Remove N95 once silica is fully incorporated
  and no dry powder is present.

STEP 4 — ADD KAOLIN CLAY:
  Slowly add 15g kaolin clay while stirring.
  Whisk until uniform. No lumps.
  Solution becomes more opaque, slightly creamy.

STEP 5 — ADD SAP (LAST):
  Slowly sprinkle 5g SAP powder over surface of solution
  while stirring continuously.
  
  Add slowly — SAP swells rapidly and can form lumps
  if added too fast.
  
  Stir continuously for 3-5 minutes.
  The gel will thicken noticeably.
  
  IF LUMPS FORM: Use immersion blender briefly to smooth.
  
  FINAL CONSISTENCY: Soft gel. Flows when tilted but
  does not pour freely. Holds shape briefly when scooped.
  Thixotropic — flows under pressure, firms when still.

STEP 6 — COOL AND STORE:
  Allow gel to cool to room temperature (20-22°C).
  Transfer to labeled airtight glass jar.
  Label: "P1S-SCROTAL — [DATE MADE] — USE BY [DATE +48HRS]"
  Refrigerate.
  
  Remove from refrigerator 15-20 minutes before use
  to allow warming toward room temperature.
  Do not microwave.
```
