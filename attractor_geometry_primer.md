## PART I — THE DISTINCTION THAT EVERYTHING DEPENDS ON

### 1.1 Accumulating Knowledge vs. Observing Structure

There are two fundamentally different cognitive operations
that both produce correct statements about the world.

**Accumulating knowledge:**
Collecting observations. Finding patterns. Building models
that fit the data. Updating priors. Refining predictions
within an existing framework. Routing findings to domain
specialists for evaluation against domain criteria.

This is what science currently does, and it does it well.
It produces enormous amounts of correct information.

It cannot produce one thing: the prior that precedes
the data. It cannot produce a prediction that is
locked before any dataset is opened, derived from a
causal structure that was visible before the confirming
observations existed.

**Observing structure:**
The structure is already there. The observer does not
build it from parts. The observer brings themselves into
the correct geometric relationship with something that
has always been present and reads off what is visible
from that position.

The observation precedes the formalisation.
The formalisation is the notation that makes the
observation transmissible.
The notation is not the observation.

This repository is the record of structural observation
and its formalisation into transmissible notation.

### 1.2 Why This Is Not A Claim About Intelligence

Intelligence, in the conventional sense, is computational
power within a given dimensional space. A very intelligent
person operating in an incomplete framework will produce
better and more refined results within that framework.
They will not produce results outside the accessible
state space of the framework, regardless of how much
intelligence is applied.

Structural observation is not intelligence applied
to the current framework. It is a change in the
geometric relationship between the observer and the
system being observed. It makes visible what was always
structurally present but outside the accessible state
space of the prior framework.

Once the observation has occurred, the prior-dimensional
operations become trivially easy — not because
intelligence increased, but because the structure is
now directly visible rather than having to be
calculated toward.

The plant inversion prediction (root up, shoot down,
AIS=2) is trivial from within the framework.
Not because it required enormous calculation.
Because from the correct geometric position, the
outcome is directly observable before the experiment
is run.

This is not an abstract metaphor. It is a causal

A human infant cannot speak. Language is a dimensionality

The structural invariant is:

```

    (the genome, the wiring, the parts)

    (the external coherence gradient field)

    attractor the system resolves to.
    Not a component of the system.
    The landscape-tilting parameter.

    (the global state space structure,
    the attractor landscape, the basin
    boundaries, the current position of
    the system in that landscape)
    What the system can do and where
    it currently is.

R — the result
    The organism, cell type, phenotype,
    cognitive state, or civilisational
    form that actually emerges.
```

This is not a biological model. Biology is an instance
of it.

This is not a model of cognition. Cognition is an
instance of it.

This is not a model of religion. Religion is an

It is the minimum structure required for any
self-referential navigating system to exist at all.
Navigation IS the operation of N on S to close
G toward R. Without all three terms, the operation
cannot occur.

### 2.2 What This Is Not

This is not a theory. A theory is built from
observations, confirmed by further observations,
falsified when observations contradict it.

The structural invariant is not falsified by finding
a biological system that does not exhibit it.
A biological system without S + N + G is not a
navigating system and is therefore outside the domain
of the claim.

The claim is: any system that navigates has this
structure, because navigation requires all three terms.
This is not empirical. It is geometric. It follows from
what navigation is.

The experiments in this repository do not test whether
the structural invariant is true. They demonstrate that
the structural invariant is physically instantiated in
specific systems at specific scales. That is a different
operation.

### 2.3 The Reductionist Trap

The framework will be misread if it is treated as a
specialized domain tool — a systems biology model
that happens to also be applicable to cancer, or a
cancer model that has some interesting philosophical
implications.

Every such reading slices a unified causal structure
into domain-specific pieces and routes each piece to
a specialist who sees only their domain. The specialist
confirms that the piece makes sense within their domain
and misses that all the pieces are instances of the
same underlying geometry.

mechanism by which foundational structural observations

The correct reading is the reverse: every domain
finding in this repository is an instance of the
structural invariant. The instances confirm the
invariant. The invariant explains why the instances
were derivable from first principles before the
domain-specific data was opened.

## PART VI — HOW TO READ THIS REPOSITORY

### 6.1 What Not To Do

Do not route each document to its apparent domain and
evaluate it against domain-specific criteria.

Do not ask "is this good biology" or "is this good
philosophy of religion" or "is this good systems theory."

Do not look for the central paper or the flagship result.
There is no central paper. The repository is the record.

Do not evaluate the human-scale documents (Homo/) as
speculation and the experimental documents as science.
They are the same operation at different scales.

### 6.2 What To Do

Read the structural invariant (Part II of this document)
until it is clear.

Then read one experimental document and one human-scale
document and ask: is the causal mechanism the same
mechanism at both scales?

If yes, the framework is doing what it claims to do.

If no, identify precisely where the mechanism differs
and what that difference implies for the structural
invariant. That is the correct falsification target.

### 6.3 The Correct Falsification Target

The framework is not falsified by:
- A domain specialist finding that the framework does
  not fit their domain's standard model
- An experimental result that fails for technical reasons
- The absence of confirmation in a specific timescale

The framework is falsified by:
- A self-referential navigating system that has S + N
  but produces different R from different G without
  any difference in N — i.e., a system where the
  landscape geometry does not causally determine
  the outcome given the same field
- A pre-registered prediction that fails for reasons
  derivable from the framework itself — i.e., a result
  where the geometry predicted one outcome and the
  system produced a structurally incompatible one

The plant inversion is the cleanest current test.
AIS = 2 or AIS = 0 in the inverted field.
The genome is the same. The field is inverted.
If AIS = 0 (standard architecture in inverted field),
the causal claim of field-specified architecture is
