# F and P Construction: A Working Record

**Status.** A working record, begun on 21 November 2026, of the designer's understanding of how F is to be constructed within the F/P workflow, and of what that understanding requires of P. It takes `boundary_board_states_engine_integration.md` as its base (§1, items 19, 20, 23 and 25; §2, readings 10, 11, 17, 20, 22 and 23) and does not change it. At the designer's direction it is kept as a separate file, so that it can be revised, folded into the other documents, or dropped without affecting them. It is stated in general terms: no piece, material, position or engine is part of it. It adds no primitive to the primitive catalog (`boundary_board_state_primitive_catalog.md`, whose admission rule is §1) and does not touch the hard core (`boundary_board_states.md` §14 items 7 to 11). The methods, goals and procedures recorded in it belong to the protective belt, and none of them is a primitive. The approach recorded here is one chosen basis, and the file does not claim that it excludes others (§1, item 55). The dependency of the priors on an engine is not a definitional influence on anything recorded here (§1, item 64). Statements marked **the designer's** are the designer's. Statements marked **this file's reading** are readings of the designer's words, offered for correction. Nothing in this file has been run, and no measurement is recorded in it. Where the understanding of the d-depth metric in this file differs from the earlier statements in the other documents, the difference is a deliberate change to the protective belt, pending empirical results (§1, item 35), and the other documents are not changed.

- **How the static measures of P combine into a rule for keeping or omitting** (§1, items 14, 31, 33 and 34; §2, readings 9, 34, 36 and 37): including how the speed tolerance is set or left to emerge, within the balance of storage, coverage and speed (item 34, reading 37).
