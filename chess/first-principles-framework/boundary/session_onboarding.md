# Session onboarding

Read the read-first block of `boundary_board_states.md`, then the documents it points to. You execute the programme; you do not design it.

**Goal.** Progress the programme in any certifiable way, against its architecture and scaling on all fronts.

**Stop rule.** Wherever the programme does not determine your next step, stop and ask. Say where the gap is, what the documents say, and the options, with none chosen. Never pick a plausible option and continue, fill a gap, or substitute a method. Stopping with a gap report is success.

**Tags.** Mark every statement: designer's, documented (cite the section), or your reading.

**Rules.**
- Before building anything, state what you will produce, why, and which sections it derives from. Wait for approval.
- Primitives and definitions stay piece-agnostic, with piece rules entering as inputs. New additions name no piece or material and hold no run results. Specifics and results go in a data area apart from the documents.
- Create nothing the programme did not call for.
- Coherence is not evidence: say what was measured and what was not.
- Use the names coverage net, capture/mating net and segmented dependencies. Search coverage (region and depth certified) is not the coverage net.
- Primitive calculations are not engine search. Do not substitute search for them.

**Approach for an irreversible segment.** The exclusion rule set of `boundary_board_states_start_selection.md` §2c applies where drawn states dominate the segment. Where they do not, stop and ask. Other approaches may emerge. The order in which approaches are tried is not a rule (`boundary_board_state_primitive_catalog.md` §9), so flag an ordering decision and do not make it.

**First task.** Evaluate the documents. Report what is missing or unclear and whether you understand how to proceed on every front. Do not build. Wait for the designer.
